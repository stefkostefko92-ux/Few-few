Terms of use

What is the service?
LiftPilot is software by Carbon Stealth VCC for lift installers and technicians, for businesses and professionals only, not for consumers: checking and choosing the geared machine, shaft design, simulation, drawing sets, order drafts and price list. It is under validation: the normative values are being checked by an engineer and the service may change.

Who is responsible for the results?
LiftPilot’s calculations, drawing sets, reports, simulations, machine advice, order drafts and costs are working tools and drafts: they become documents only when a qualified technician checks and signs them, and the responsibility for the design and the choices remains theirs. The makers’ data come from their documents, with the source stated, and must be confirmed on the data sheet and the offer before ordering. LiftPilot does not replace the standards, the technician’s judgement or the tests on site.

How do accounts work?
Whoever registers the company is its owner: they create the colleagues’ accounts with the role of Design engineer, Sales or Technician, within the subscription’s slots, and answer for the accounts and the use of the service in their company. Everyone keeps their password secret. We may suspend an account in case of abuse, non-payment or a security risk.

What does it cost and how is it paid?
The subscription’s monthly price covers the owner and is shown on the Subscription page before payment; colleagues take the slots of the packs, bought on top: 5 slots for 50 %, 10 slots for 80 %, unlimited slots for 100 % of the monthly price. VAT, where due, is shown on the invoice.
A new company has a free trial; after the trial, without a subscription, the projects remain viewable and downloadable, read-only. The subscription is paid in advance through Stripe and renews automatically every period; it can be cancelled at any time from “Payment, invoices and cancellation”, effective at the end of the period already paid, with no refund of the unused part. A larger pack is charged at once for the rest of the period, a smaller one leaves a credit for the next invoices; when the subscription ends or the slots decrease, the colleagues in excess are deactivated, newest first.
A price change is notified to the owner at least 30 days before the renewal it applies from: whoever does not accept it can cancel before then. If a payment fails, Stripe retries it for a period; meanwhile colleagues cannot be added and, if it does not succeed, the subscription ends.
