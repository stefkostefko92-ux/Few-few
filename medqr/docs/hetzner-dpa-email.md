# Имейл до Hetzner за Договор за обработка на данни (DPA / AVV)

Когато използвате Hetzner като хостинг за лични (и още повече — здравни) данни,
по GDPR (чл. 28) сте длъжни да сключите **Договор за обработка на данни** с тях
(на немски: *Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag, AVV*; на английски: *Data Processing
Agreement, DPA*).

> Бележка: Hetzner обикновено предоставя DPA-то и за **електронно сключване**
> директно през акаунта ви (Hetzner Cloud Console / Robot → раздел за защита на
> данните / „Order processing"). Първо проверете там — може да го подпишете онлайн
> без имейл. Ако не го намерите, изпратете писмото по-долу.

Адрес: **info@hetzner.com** (или през формата за поддръжка в акаунта).
Тема: **Request for Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) — Customer No. <вашия номер>**

## Вариант на английски

Subject: Request for Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) — Customer No. <your customer number>

Dear Hetzner Team,

I am a Hetzner customer (customer number: <your number>) using your services to host
an application that processes personal data, including special-category health data
under Article 9 GDPR. As the data controller, I need to put in place a Data Processing
Agreement (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag) with Hetzner as the data processor pursuant
to Article 28 GDPR.

Could you please:

1. Provide the current Data Processing Agreement (DPA/AVV) for signature, or point me
   to where I can conclude it electronically in my account.
2. Confirm that the data will be stored and processed exclusively in your data centers
   in Germany (EU).
3. Provide your current list of sub-processors.
4. Provide a summary of the technical and organizational measures (TOMs) under
   Article 32 GDPR, and any relevant certifications (e.g. ISO/IEC 27001).
5. Confirm whether any data transfers outside the EU/EEA take place and, if so, on what
   legal basis (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

Company / controller details for the agreement:
- Controller: CarbonStealth VCC
- Website: https://carbonstealth.eu
- Contact e-mail: <your contact e-mail>
- Address: <your registered address>

Thank you in advance. I look forward to your reply.
