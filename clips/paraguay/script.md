## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Paraguay is one of South America's best-kept secrets — a landlocked nation at the
continent's heart, where most people still speak the indigenous Guaraní language.
Here you'll find romantic Jesuit ruins, vast wetlands and one of the largest dams
on Earth. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Paraguay. Let's count
them down.
*B-roll: montage — Asunción riverfront, Jesuit ruins, wetlands, red-white-blue flags.*

### #10 — Palacio de los López (Asunción)
We start in the capital. The Palacio de los López is Paraguay's elegant
presidential palace, a grand 19th-century building overlooking the Paraguay River.
Beautifully lit at night, it's the political and architectural centrepiece of
Asunción.
*B-roll: palace façade, riverfront, night illumination.*

### #9 — Panteón Nacional de los Héroes (Asunción)
The nation's shrine of remembrance. Modelled on Les Invalides in Paris, this
domed monument in the heart of Asunción holds the tombs of Paraguay's national
heroes and is guarded around the clock. A small but proud symbol of the country.
*B-roll: domed monument, guards, city square.*

### #8 — Ñeembucú & Pilar
A region of rivers and wetlands in the south. Around the town of Pilar, the
Ñeembucú wetlands teem with birds, capybaras and caimans, and the slow rhythm of
river life feels worlds away. A paradise for nature and birdwatching.
*B-roll: wetlands at sunrise, wading birds, river boats.*

### #7 — Cerro Corá National Park
History and wild scenery combine here. Cerro Corá protects dramatic rocky hills
and dry forest in the northeast — and marks the site where the War of the Triple
Alliance ended. Today it's a place to hike, spot wildlife and explore ancient
rock carvings.
*B-roll: rocky hills, forest trails, monuments.*

### #6 — Lake Ypacaraí & San Bernardino
Paraguay's favourite summer escape. The lakeside resort town of San Bernardino,
on the shores of Lake Ypacaraí, has long been the playground of Asunción.
Immortalised in a famous Guaraní song, it's all beaches, boats and laid-back
holiday vibes.
*B-roll: lakeside beach, boats, summer crowds.*

### #5 — Filadelfia & the Chaco
Into the wild west. The Chaco is a vast, sparsely populated wilderness covering
much of Paraguay, and the town of Filadelfia is the heart of its surprising
Mennonite communities. Beyond it lies one of South America's last great frontiers,
home to jaguars, armadillos and endless dry forest.
*B-roll: dusty frontier town, Chaco wilderness, wildlife.*

### #4 — Saltos del Monday
A waterfall in the shadow of its famous neighbour. Near Ciudad del Este, the
Saltos del Monday thunder over a 40-metre drop in a horseshoe of falls. Less
crowded than nearby Iguazú, with walkways that bring you right to the edge, it's a
spectacular sight in its own right.
*B-roll: horseshoe falls, walkways, mist and rainbows.*

### #3 — The Río Paraguay & Northern Wetlands
The great river that gives the country its name. The Río Paraguay flows into the
southern reaches of the vast Pantanal wetland, where boat journeys reveal
caimans, capybaras, giant river otters and a sky full of birds. The country's
wild, watery heart.
*B-roll: river journey, caimans, capybaras, flocks of birds.*

### #2 — Itaipú Dam
One of the largest power stations on the planet. The Itaipú Dam, shared with
Brazil on the Paraná River, is an engineering colossus that for years generated
more electricity than any other dam on Earth. Tours take you across its massive
structure — and at times, the spillway gates unleash a breathtaking flood of
water.
*B-roll: vast dam wall, open spillway, night light show.*

### #1 — The Jesuit Missions of Trinidad & Jesús
And our number one is Paraguay's crown jewel. The ruins of La Santísima Trinidad
de Paraná and Jesús de Tavarangue are UNESCO World Heritage Sites — beautifully
preserved red-stone remains of 18th-century Jesuit missions, with carved church
façades rising from the green countryside. Romantic, peaceful and historic. If you
see one place in Paraguay, make it these.
*B-roll: red-stone ruins, carved façades, drone over the mission, sunset glow.*
