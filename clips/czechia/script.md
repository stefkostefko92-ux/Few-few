## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Czechia — the Czech Republic, right in the heart of Europe and through to the World
Cup via the play-offs. This is a land of fairytale castles and golden cities,
storybook towns, healing spas and the best beer on the planet. Here are the Top 10
landmarks you have to see in Czechia. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Prague skyline, castles, Český Krumlov, blue-white-red flags.*

### #10 — Pilsen & Czech Beer
We start with a national treasure. The city of Pilsen gave the world Pilsner — the
golden lager that changed beer forever — and you can tour the historic Pilsner
Urquell brewery and its underground cellars. In Czechia, beer is culture.
*B-roll: brewery, golden beer poured, cellars, town square.*

### #9 — Telč
A Renaissance jewel. The UNESCO town of Telč is famous for its picture-perfect main
square, lined with pastel-coloured houses and arched arcades, beside a fairytale
château and ponds. One of the prettiest squares in Europe.
*B-roll: pastel arcaded square, château, reflecting ponds.*

### #8 — Karlovy Vary
The grand spa town. Karlovy Vary is a romantic valley of elegant colonnades and
candy-coloured buildings, built around hot mineral springs where visitors sip
healing waters from special cups. Glamorous and beautiful, especially in autumn.
*B-roll: colonnades, hot springs, riverside spa buildings.*

### #7 — Bohemian Switzerland
A wonderland of rock. This national park on the German border is a landscape of
deep gorges, pine forests and dramatic sandstone formations — crowned by the
Pravčická brána, the largest natural stone arch in Europe. Magical hiking country.
*B-roll: the great stone arch, gorge boat ride, forested cliffs.*

### #6 — Kutná Hora
A town of silver and bone. Once one of the richest towns in Europe from its silver
mines, Kutná Hora boasts the soaring Gothic St Barbara's Cathedral — and the eerie
Sedlec Ossuary, a chapel decorated with the bones of tens of thousands of people.
Beautiful and haunting.
*B-roll: St Barbara's Cathedral, bone chandelier of the ossuary.*

### #5 — Karlštejn Castle
A royal Gothic fortress. Founded by Holy Roman Emperor Charles IV, Karlštejn rises
in tiers on a wooded hill above a village, once guarding the crown jewels. One of
the most famous and majestic castles in the country.
*B-roll: hilltop castle towers, forest, village below.*

### #4 — Český Krumlov
A fairytale come to life. This UNESCO town curls inside a tight bend of the Vltava
river, a maze of red-roofed medieval houses watched over by a vast castle with a
painted tower. Utterly enchanting from every angle.
*B-roll: river bend and red roofs, painted castle tower, cobbled lanes.*

### #3 — Old Town Square & the Astronomical Clock, Prague
The beating heart of Prague. The Old Town Square is ringed by Gothic spires, baroque
palaces and pastel houses — and every hour, crowds gather beneath the medieval
Astronomical Clock to watch its figures and apostles spring to life. Over 600 years
old and still ticking.
*B-roll: the square, twin Gothic spires, the clock's hourly show.*

### #2 — Charles Bridge, Prague
The most beautiful bridge in the city. The 14th-century Charles Bridge strides
across the Vltava on stone arches, lined with rows of baroque statues, street
musicians and artists, with Prague Castle rising beyond. At dawn, almost empty and
utterly magical.
*B-roll: statue-lined bridge, castle backdrop, dawn light, musicians.*

### #1 — Prague Castle
And our number one is the largest ancient castle complex in the world. Prague
Castle has crowned the hill above the city for over a thousand years — a vast
ensemble dominated by the soaring Gothic spires of St Vitus Cathedral, with palaces,
gardens and the charming Golden Lane within. The glittering symbol of Czechia. If
you see one thing here, make it this.
*B-roll: St Vitus spires, castle panorama over Prague, Golden Lane, changing guard.*
