## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Côte d'Ivoire — the Ivory Coast, reigning champions of Africa and a country of
striking contrasts. Gleaming modern skylines, the largest church on Earth, sacred
lakes, lush mountains and golden surf beaches. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you
have to see in Côte d'Ivoire. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Abidjan skyline, the great basilica, beaches, orange-white-green flags.*

### #10 — St Paul's Cathedral, Abidjan
We start with bold modern architecture. The St Paul's Cathedral in Abidjan is a
dramatic, futuristic design — a sweeping white structure where the roof is held by
a giant figure leaning over the nave. A striking landmark of the economic capital.
*B-roll: futuristic cathedral, sweeping roof, stained glass.*

### #9 — Grand-Bassam
The historic first capital. This UNESCO-listed colonial town has faded French
buildings, an artisan quarter and a long Atlantic beach. Once the heart of the
colony, today it's a relaxed, atmospheric seaside escape near Abidjan.
*B-roll: colonial facades, craft market, beach.*

### #8 — Banco National Park
A rainforest inside the city. Remarkably, Abidjan wraps around Banco National Park,
a patch of dense primary rainforest with towering trees and walking trails — a
green lung right beside the bustling metropolis.
*B-roll: tall rainforest trees, jungle trail, city nearby.*

### #7 — Comoé National Park
One of West Africa's largest reserves. This vast UNESCO savannah park along the
Comoé River shelters elephants, antelopes, chimpanzees and hundreds of bird
species across a huge range of habitats. Wild and remote.
*B-roll: savannah, river, elephants, birds.*

### #6 — Man & the Eighteen Mountains
Green peaks and waterfalls. The region around Man, in the west, is a lush highland
of forested mountains, waterfalls and the famous "tooth" peak of Mount Tonkoui.
Known for its vine bridges and mask traditions. Cool, scenic and beautiful.
*B-roll: green mountains, waterfall, liana vine bridge.*

### #5 — The Sacred Crocodile Lakes, Yamoussoukro
Legendary reptiles in the capital. Around the presidential palace in Yamoussoukro
lie lakes famous for their resident sacred crocodiles, a long-standing local
tradition and curiosity. A quirky, memorable stop.
*B-roll: lakeside, crocodiles, palace in the distance.*

### #4 — The Beaches of Assouindé & Assinie
Tropical coastline. East of Abidjan, the palm-fringed beaches around Assinie and
Assouindé, set between ocean and lagoon, are the country's favourite weekend
escape — golden sand, watersports and laid-back resorts.
*B-roll: palm beach, lagoon, watersports, resorts.*

### #3 — The Plateau District, Abidjan
The "Manhattan of West Africa." Abidjan's Plateau bristles with modern skyscrapers
rising above the lagoon — the gleaming, energetic business heart of one of West
Africa's most dynamic cities. Best seen at sunset from across the water.
*B-roll: skyline over the lagoon, skyscrapers, sunset.*

### #2 — Taï National Park
Primeval rainforest. Taï is one of the last great tracts of pristine rainforest in
West Africa, a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its rare wildlife — including
chimpanzees known to use stone tools. Dense, ancient and precious.
*B-roll: thick rainforest, chimpanzees, river, canopy.*

### #1 — The Basilica of Our Lady of Peace, Yamoussoukro
And our number one is a record-breaker. Rising astonishingly from the savannah, the
Basilica of Our Lady of Peace is the largest church in the world, its dome
modelled on St Peter's in Rome but built even taller. Surrounded by marble plazas
and columns, its scale is simply staggering. If you see one place in Côte d'Ivoire,
make it this.
*B-roll: vast domed basilica, marble colonnades, interior, aerial.*
