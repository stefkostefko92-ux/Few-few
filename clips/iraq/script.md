## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Iraq — the cradle of civilisation itself. This is ancient Mesopotamia, the "land
between the rivers," where humanity built its first cities, invented writing and
raised mighty ziggurats. From legendary Babylon to bustling Baghdad, here are the
Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Iraq. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Erbil citadel, spiral minaret, marshes, red-white-black flags.*

### #10 — Mutanabbi Street, Baghdad
We start in the capital. Mutanabbi Street is the historic heart of Baghdad's
literary life — a famous book market where stalls overflow with books and
intellectuals gather in old cafés like the Shabandar. The soul of a city that has
loved learning for over a thousand years.
*B-roll: book stalls, old café, busy street.*

### #9 — Lalish
A sacred valley. Tucked in the northern mountains, Lalish is the holiest temple of
the Yazidi people, a peaceful place of conical spires, sacred springs and ancient
courtyards entered barefoot. Tranquil and deeply spiritual.
*B-roll: conical temple spires, courtyards, springs (respectful tone).*

### #8 — Hatra
A desert city that defied Rome. The UNESCO ruins of Hatra rise from the desert with
grand temples and columns blending Greek, Roman and Eastern styles. Once a powerful
caravan city, its circular walls twice repelled the Roman legions. Majestic and
remote.
*B-roll: temple ruins, columns, desert, carved façades.*

### #7 — The Shrines of Najaf & Karbala
Among the holiest sites in Shia Islam. The golden-domed Imam Ali Shrine in Najaf and
the Imam Husayn Shrine in Karbala dazzle with gold, mirrors and intricate tilework,
drawing millions of pilgrims each year in some of the largest gatherings on Earth.
*B-roll: golden domes, mirrored interiors, vast courtyards (respectful tone).*

### #6 — The Al-Mustansiriya Madrasah, Baghdad
One of the oldest universities in the world. Founded in 1227 on the banks of the
Tigris, this beautiful medieval madrasah of brick arches and courtyards was a great
centre of learning in the golden age of Baghdad. History you can walk through.
*B-roll: brick courtyard, arched halls, river nearby.*

### #5 — The Mesopotamian Marshes
The legendary "Garden of Eden." In Iraq's south, the UNESCO-listed marshes are a
vast wetland of reeds and waterways, home to the Marsh Arabs who live in houses
woven from reeds and travel by canoe — a way of life thousands of years old. Unique
and beautiful.
*B-roll: reed houses, canoes through reeds, water buffalo, birds.*

### #4 — Samarra & the Malwiya Minaret
A spiral against the sky. The great mosque of Samarra is famous for its Malwiya
Minaret — an extraordinary spiral tower that you climb by a ramp winding around the
outside, over 50 metres high. A UNESCO icon of early Islamic architecture, unlike
anything else.
*B-roll: spiral minaret, the ramp, desert town, aerial.*

### #3 — The Ziggurat of Ur
A pyramid from the dawn of history. The Great Ziggurat of Ur is a massive stepped
temple-mound built over 4,000 years ago by the Sumerians, beautifully restored with
its grand staircases. Standing before it, you feel the very beginnings of
civilisation.
*B-roll: stepped ziggurat, grand staircase, ancient city ruins.*

### #2 — Babylon
One of the most legendary cities in history. The ruins of Babylon — once home to
the fabled Hanging Gardens and the great Ishtar Gate — spread across the desert near
the Euphrates, with restored walls, the famous Lion of Babylon statue and echoes of
kings like Nebuchadnezzar and Hammurabi. A UNESCO World Heritage Site of true legend.
*B-roll: reconstructed walls, blue-tiled gate replica, Lion of Babylon.*

### #1 — The Citadel of Erbil
And our number one may be the oldest continuously inhabited place on Earth. The
Citadel of Erbil rises on a great mound above the city, layer upon layer of human
settlement stretching back some 6,000 years, crowned by a UNESCO-listed old town of
mosques, mansions and winding lanes. To stand here is to stand on history itself.
If you see one place in Iraq, make it this.
*B-roll: citadel mound over the city, old town lanes, gateway, bazaar below.*
