## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Panama — the narrow bridge between two continents and two oceans. Home to one of the
engineering wonders of the world, a gleaming modern skyline, island paradises and
some of the richest rainforest on Earth. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to
see in Panama. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — canal locks, city skyline, island palms, red-white-blue flags.*

### #10 — The Panama City Skyline
We start in the capital. Panama City has the most dramatic skyline in Central
America — a forest of glass towers, including the corkscrew-shaped F&F Tower, rising
along the Pacific bay. Stroll the Cinta Costera waterfront for the full view.
*B-roll: skyline across the bay, the twisted tower, waterfront.*

### #9 — Casco Viejo & the Biomuseo
Old and new side by side. Casco Viejo, the UNESCO old town, charms with colonial
plazas, churches and rooftop bars. Out on the causeway stands the Biomuseo — a
riot of colourful folded roofs designed by Frank Gehry, telling how Panama's land
bridge changed the planet.
*B-roll: colonial streets, plazas, the colourful Biomuseo.*

### #8 — Portobelo
A Caribbean fortress town. Once the port where Spain shipped its New World gold,
Portobelo is a UNESCO site of crumbling stone forts and cannons overlooking a
turquoise bay, steeped in history and Afro-Caribbean culture.
*B-roll: stone fort and cannons, bay, colourful town.*

### #7 — The Pearl Islands
An archipelago of paradise. The Pearl Islands scatter across the Pacific with
white-sand beaches and clear water, famous for whale watching — and as a filming
location for survival TV. Easy island-hopping from the capital.
*B-roll: white beaches, clear water, whales, boats.*

### #6 — Soberanía National Park
Rainforest at the city's edge. Along the canal, Soberanía is a birdwatcher's
paradise — toucans, parrots and hundreds of species — with jungle trails and boat
trips on Gatún Lake to spot monkeys and sloths. Wild nature minutes from the towers.
*B-roll: rainforest canopy, toucans, monkeys, lake boat.*

### #5 — Volcán Barú
The roof of Panama. The country's highest point, the Barú volcano, soars over the
western highlands — and on a clear dawn from its summit, you can see both the
Pacific and the Caribbean at once. A challenging, rewarding climb.
*B-roll: volcano summit, sunrise, both coastlines in the haze.*

### #4 — Boquete
Cool green highlands. Nestled in misty mountains, the town of Boquete is famous for
its world-class coffee farms, flower gardens, hot springs and adventure — from
hiking and zip-lining to rafting. A refreshing escape from the tropical heat.
*B-roll: coffee farms, misty mountains, flowers, zip-line.*

### #3 — Bocas del Toro
Caribbean island life. This laid-back archipelago is a backpacker and beach
favourite — turquoise water, overwater bungalows, surf breaks, mangroves and
dolphins, all wrapped in a relaxed island vibe. Pure Caribbean Panama.
*B-roll: overwater bungalows, turquoise sea, surf, dolphins.*

### #2 — The San Blas Islands (Guna Yala)
A self-governing island paradise. The San Blas archipelago is made up of hundreds
of tiny palm-topped islands run by the indigenous Guna people, with white sand,
crystal water and traditional culture. Some islands are barely bigger than a beach
towel. Idyllic.
*B-roll: tiny palm islands, clear water, Guna sailing boats, molas.*

### #1 — The Panama Canal
And our number one is one of the greatest engineering feats in human history.
The Panama Canal cuts across the country to link the Atlantic and Pacific oceans,
lifting giant ships up and over the continent through massive locks. Watch a
colossal cargo ship rise before your eyes at the Miraflores Locks — it's
unforgettable. If you see one thing in Panama, make it this.
*B-roll: ship in the locks, gates closing, observation deck, aerial of the canal.*
