## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
New Zealand — Aotearoa, a land so beautiful it became Middle-earth on screen.
Towering fjords and glaciers, steaming geysers, turquoise lakes and snow-capped
peaks, all wrapped in rich Māori culture. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to
see in New Zealand. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Milford Sound, Mount Cook, geysers, silver-fern flags.*

### #10 — The Sky Tower, Auckland
We start in the biggest city. The Sky Tower soars 328 metres over Auckland, the
tallest building in the Southern Hemisphere, with a glass-floored observation deck —
and a SkyJump for the brave. Sweeping views over the "City of Sails" and its
harbours.
*B-roll: the tower, glass floor, harbour view, SkyJump.*

### #9 — The Waitomo Glowworm Caves
An underground galaxy. In the Waitomo caves, thousands of tiny glowworms light up
the ceiling like a starry sky. Glide silently through the dark on a boat beneath
their blue-green glow. Utterly magical.
*B-roll: glowworm-lit cave ceiling, boat on dark water.*

### #8 — Rotorua's Geothermal Wonderland
Where the earth bubbles and steams. Rotorua is a hotbed of geothermal activity —
erupting geysers, bright orange and green hot springs and bubbling mud pools — and a
heartland of Māori culture, with traditional villages and performances.
*B-roll: geyser erupting, colourful hot springs, Māori performance.*

### #7 — Hobbiton
Step into Middle-earth. The rolling green hills near Matamata are home to the
Hobbiton movie set, with its round hobbit-hole doors, the Green Dragon Inn and the
Party Tree, kept exactly as in the films. A must for fans and families.
*B-roll: hobbit holes, green hills, the Green Dragon Inn.*

### #6 — The Tongariro Alpine Crossing
One of the best day hikes in the world. This volcanic trek crosses a dramatic
landscape of craters, lava fields and vivid Emerald Lakes, past the cone of Mount
Ngauruhoe — which played Mount Doom on screen. Raw, otherworldly beauty.
*B-roll: emerald crater lakes, volcanic peaks, hikers on the ridge.*

### #5 — Franz Josef & Fox Glaciers
Rivers of ice in the rainforest. On the West Coast, these dramatic glaciers tumble
from the Southern Alps almost down to sea level, meeting lush green forest. Take a
helicopter to walk on the ice — a rare and spectacular sight.
*B-roll: glacier from the air, ice walk, rainforest below.*

### #4 — Lake Tekapo & Mount Cook
Turquoise water beneath the highest peak. Lake Tekapo glows an impossible milky
blue, with the little Church of the Good Shepherd on its shore and Aoraki / Mount
Cook — New Zealand's tallest mountain — rising beyond. One of the world's great dark-
sky stargazing spots.
*B-roll: turquoise lake, lupins, the lone church, Mount Cook, night stars.*

### #3 — Queenstown
The adventure capital of the world. Set on a stunning lake beneath the jagged
Remarkables mountains, Queenstown is the home of bungee jumping and a hub for
skiing, jet boating and skydiving — plus relaxed lakeside dining. Beauty and
adrenaline combined.
*B-roll: lake and mountains, bungee jump, jet boat, gondola view.*

### #2 — The Bay of Islands
A subtropical paradise in the north. The Bay of Islands scatters 144 islands across
sparkling blue water, alive with dolphins and famous for sailing, sea kayaking and
the historic "Hole in the Rock." Also the birthplace of modern New Zealand at
Waitangi.
*B-roll: islands and blue water, dolphins, sailing, Hole in the Rock.*

### #1 — Milford Sound
And our number one is the "eighth wonder of the world." Milford Sound is a
breathtaking fjord where sheer cliffs plunge thousands of metres into dark water,
laced with waterfalls and crowned by the peak of Mitre Peak. Cruise it in the rain
and a thousand cascades appear. Simply sublime. If you see one thing in New Zealand,
make it this.
*B-roll: fjord cruise, Mitre Peak, waterfalls, seals, mist.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the wild golden beaches of Abel Tasman, and the artsy capital of
Wellington with its cable car and harbour. That's the Top 10 landmarks of New
Zealand. Which one would you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every
nation at the 2026 World Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: Abel Tasman coast, Wellington cable car, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
