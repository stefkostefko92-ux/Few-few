## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
France — the most visited country on the planet, and a feast for the senses.
Grand palaces and fairytale châteaux, lavender fields and alpine peaks, world-class
food and the most romantic city on Earth. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have
to see in France. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Eiffel Tower, châteaux, lavender, French tricolour flags.*

### #10 — Carcassonne
We start in the south with a real-life fairytale. Carcassonne is a perfectly
preserved medieval fortified city, ringed by double stone walls and 52 towers.
Wander its cobbled lanes and ramparts and you'll feel as though you've stepped
straight into the Middle Ages.
*B-roll: turreted walls, drawbridge, cobbled lanes within.*

### #9 — Pont du Gard
A masterpiece of Roman engineering. The Pont du Gard is a colossal three-tiered
aqueduct bridge built nearly 2,000 years ago to carry water across a river valley.
Standing 49 metres high, beautifully preserved, it's one of the finest Roman
monuments anywhere.
*B-roll: the three-tier aqueduct, river below, arches close up.*

### #8 — The French Riviera (Côte d'Azur)
Glamour by the Mediterranean. The Côte d'Azur strings together Nice's pebble
beaches, the film-festival glitz of Cannes and the millionaire's playground of
Saint-Tropez, all along a coast of brilliant blue. Sun, style and azure sea.
*B-roll: Nice Promenade des Anglais, yachts, coastal villages.*

### #7 — Provence & the Lavender Fields
The scent of summer. In Provence, endless rows of purple lavender roll across the
landscape beneath golden light, dotted with stone villages and Roman towns. The
plateau de Valensole in bloom is one of the most photographed sights in France.
*B-roll: purple lavender rows, stone villages, bees and light.*

### #6 — Mont Blanc & Chamonix
The roof of Western Europe. At 4,800 metres, Mont Blanc towers over the alpine
town of Chamonix. Ride the cable car to the Aiguille du Midi for a heart-stopping
view of glaciers and peaks — then step into the glass "Step into the Void" box
suspended over a 1,000-metre drop.
*B-roll: snowy summit, cable car, glass skywalk over the void.*

### #5 — The Châteaux of the Loire Valley
A valley of fairytale palaces. The Loire is lined with hundreds of Renaissance
châteaux — the moated Chenonceau arching over its river, the vast Chambord with
its double-helix staircase said to be designed by Leonardo da Vinci. Royal France
at its most romantic.
*B-roll: Chenonceau over the river, Chambord's roofline, formal gardens.*

### #4 — Notre-Dame de Paris
A Gothic masterpiece reborn. For over 850 years Notre-Dame has stood on its island
in the Seine, famous for its rose windows, flying buttresses and gargoyles.
Lovingly restored after the 2019 fire, this cathedral remains the spiritual heart
of Paris.
*B-roll: cathedral façade, rose window, gargoyles, the Seine.*

### #3 — The Palace of Versailles
The most extravagant palace in Europe. Built by the Sun King, Louis XIV,
Versailles dazzles with the mirror-lined Hall of Mirrors, gilded royal apartments
and vast formal gardens with their musical fountains. A monument to absolute
power and breathtaking excess.
*B-roll: Hall of Mirrors, golden gates, gardens and fountains from above.*

### #2 — Mont Saint-Michel
One of the most magical sights in France. This medieval abbey rises on a rocky
island off the Normandy coast, its spire soaring above the bay. At high tide it's
ringed by the sea; at low tide, by vast sand flats. Mystical and unforgettable.
*B-roll: abbey island at high tide, causeway, spire against the sky.*

### #1 — The Eiffel Tower (Paris)
And our number one is the symbol of France and one of the most recognisable
structures on Earth. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, the Eiffel Tower soars 330
metres over Paris. Climb it for a view across the rooftops, or watch it sparkle
with thousands of lights every hour after dark. If you see one landmark in France,
make it this.
*B-roll: tower from the Trocadéro, view from the top, the nightly sparkle.*
