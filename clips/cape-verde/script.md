## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Cape Verde — making history with their first-ever World Cup. The Blue Sharks come
from a breathtaking volcanic archipelago off the coast of West Africa, where every
island has its own character: dramatic volcanoes, endless dunes, soulful music and
some of the Atlantic's best beaches. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see
in Cape Verde. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — island beaches, the volcano, colourful Mindelo, blue-white flags.*

### #10 — Praia & Santiago Island
We start in the capital. Praia, on the largest island of Santiago, is the lively
hub of Cape Verde, with a colourful plateau old town, busy markets and a string of
nearby beaches. The cultural and political centre of the nation.
*B-roll: plateau old town, market, city beaches.*

### #9 — Cidade Velha
The first European city in the tropics. This UNESCO-listed old town near Praia was
the original capital, with a ruined cathedral, an old fort on the hill and the
oldest colonial church in the world still standing. Beautiful and historic.
*B-roll: hilltop fort, cathedral ruins, cobbled street.*

### #8 — Mindelo, São Vicente
The cultural capital. Mindelo, set around a pretty harbour, is the birthplace of
Cape Verdean music — the home of the legendary singer Cesária Évora — and famous for
its colourful carnival. Vibrant, musical and full of soul.
*B-roll: colourful harbour town, music bars, carnival.*

### #7 — The Beaches of Sal
Endless golden sand. The flat island of Sal is Cape Verde's beach capital, with
long stretches of white sand and turquoise water at Santa Maria, ideal for
swimming, kitesurfing and watching the fishing boats come in.
*B-roll: white beach, turquoise water, kitesurfers, fishing pier.*

### #6 — The Pedra de Lume Salt Crater
Float in a volcano. On Sal, an old volcanic crater has been flooded to form salt
pools so dense you float effortlessly, just like the Dead Sea — a surreal and fun
natural spa surrounded by crater walls.
*B-roll: salt pools in the crater, floating swimmers, white salt.*

### #5 — The Dunes of Boa Vista
A slice of the Sahara in the Atlantic. Boa Vista is ringed by some of the longest
beaches in the country and rolling desert dunes inland, where turtles nest and
shipwrecks rust on the sand. Wild and beautiful.
*B-roll: desert dunes, vast empty beach, a shipwreck, turtles.*

### #4 — Tarrafal Beach
The classic postcard beach. On the northern tip of Santiago, Tarrafal offers a
gorgeous crescent of white sand and calm blue water backed by palms and green
hills. Many call it the most beautiful beach on the island.
*B-roll: crescent beach, palms, blue water, fishing boats.*

### #3 — The Cova Crater & Paúl Valley
A green oasis. On Santo Antão, an old volcanic crater gives way to the lush,
terraced Paúl Valley, tumbling down to the sea in ribbons of green — sugarcane,
banana and flowers. A stunning contrast to the dry islands.
*B-roll: green crater, terraced valley to the sea, lush slopes.*

### #2 — The Trails of Santo Antão
A hiker's paradise. Santo Antão is the most dramatic island, a land of towering
peaks, deep ravines and stone footpaths winding along cliff edges between tiny
villages. Some of the most spectacular hiking trails in the Atlantic.
*B-roll: cliff-edge trail, deep ravines, mountain villages.*

### #1 — Pico do Fogo
And our number one is the giant of the archipelago. Pico do Fogo is an active
volcano rising nearly 2,800 metres straight out of the ocean on the island of Fogo
— with a village living inside its vast crater, growing vines in the black volcanic
soil to make a famous local wine. Climbing it is an unforgettable adventure. If you
see one thing in Cape Verde, make it this.
*B-roll: the volcanic cone, the crater village, black-soil vineyards, summit climb.*

A couple more: the laid-back island of Maio with its quiet beaches, and the whale
and turtle watching across the islands in season. That's the Top 10 landmarks of
