## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Curaçao — history-makers, the smallest nation ever to reach a World Cup. This
Dutch-Caribbean island is a jewel of colourful colonial streets, hidden coves of
impossibly blue water and rugged wild coastlines. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you
have to see in Curaçao. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — colourful Willemstad, turquoise coves, flamingos, blue flags.*

### #10 — The Queen Emma Bridge, Willemstad
We start with a floating landmark. The Queen Emma Bridge is a unique pontoon bridge
that swings open across the harbour to let ships pass, connecting the two halves of
the capital. Locals call it the "Swinging Old Lady." A beloved, one-of-a-kind sight.
*B-roll: pontoon bridge, swinging open, harbour, colourful facades behind.*

### #9 — Mambo Beach
The lively heart of beach life. Mambo Beach is a buzzing stretch of white sand,
turquoise water, bars and a boulevard near the capital — the easy, fun introduction
to Curaçao's coast. Snorkel right off the shore.
*B-roll: white beach, turquoise water, beach bars, snorkelling.*

### #8 — The Hato Caves
An underground world. The Hato Caves are a network of limestone caverns with
stalactites, underground pools, ancient indigenous rock drawings and a colony of
fruit bats. A cool, fascinating escape from the sun.
*B-roll: cave formations, underground pools, rock drawings.*

### #7 — The Flamingos of Jan Kok
A flash of pink. The salt flats around Jan Kok and Sint Willibrordus are home to
flocks of wild Caribbean flamingos, wading and feeding in the shallow lagoons. A
beautiful, colourful sight against the salt pans.
*B-roll: pink flamingos in a lagoon, salt flats.*

### #6 — Klein Curaçao
An uninhabited desert island. A boat ride away lies Klein Curaçao — "Little
Curaçao" — a flat, sandy island with a lonely lighthouse, a shipwreck, sea turtles
and some of the clearest water in the Caribbean. A perfect day-trip paradise.
*B-roll: empty white island, lighthouse, turtles, clear water.*

### #5 — Shete Boka National Park
Where the ocean roars. On the wild north coast, Shete Boka protects a series of
rocky inlets where the Atlantic crashes into caves and blowholes, sending spray
high into the air. Dramatic and powerful.
*B-roll: waves crashing into inlets, blowholes, rugged coast.*

### #4 — The Blue Room Cave
A glowing sea cave. Near Westpunt, the Blue Room is a partly submerged cave where
sunlight filters through the water to fill it with an electric blue glow. Snorkel or
swim inside for a magical experience. Unforgettable.
*B-roll: glowing blue cave interior, snorkeller, light through water.*

### #3 — Christoffel National Park
The island's wild heart. This national park is crowned by Mount Christoffel, the
highest peak on the island, with hiking trails, cacti, rare wild orchids and
roaming deer. Climb to the summit for sweeping views across the whole island.
*B-roll: mountain trail, cacti, deer, summit panorama.*

### #2 — Playa Kenepa (Knip)
The most beautiful beach on the island. Playa Kenepa Grandi is a postcard cove of
powder-white sand and brilliant turquoise water framed by cliffs — perfect for
swimming, snorkelling and the famous cliff jump. Pure Caribbean perfection.
*B-roll: turquoise cove from the cliff, white sand, cliff jumpers.*

### #1 — Willemstad & the Handelskade
And our number one is the island's icon. Willemstad's UNESCO old town is famous for
the Handelskade — a waterfront row of Dutch colonial buildings painted in every
candy colour, reflected in the harbour. With its districts of Punda and Otrobanda,
floating market and lively streets, it's one of the most photogenic capitals in the
Caribbean. If you see one place in Curaçao, make it this.
*B-roll: colourful waterfront, harbour reflections, floating market, streets.*
