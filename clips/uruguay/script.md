## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Uruguay — the nation that won the very first World Cup back in 1930, and still one
of football's proudest countries. Small, safe and wonderfully relaxed, it offers
historic ports, glamorous beaches and endless gaucho plains. Here are the Top 10
landmarks you have to see in Uruguay. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Montevideo rambla, beaches, the hand sculpture, sky-blue flags.*

### #10 — Estadio Centenario (Montevideo)
We start at a temple of football. The Estadio Centenario hosted and won the
inaugural 1930 World Cup final, and FIFA has named it a Historical Monument of
World Football. Inside is a museum dedicated to Uruguay's golden footballing past
— essential for any fan visiting in a World Cup year.
*B-roll: stadium exterior, the tower, football museum.*

### #9 — Mercado del Puerto (Montevideo)
The tastiest landmark in the capital. This wrought-iron market hall by the old
port is filled with parrillas — open grills loaded with Uruguay's famous beef.
The smoke, the sizzle and the buzz make it the perfect place to taste why Uruguay
is one of the great steak nations.
*B-roll: grills loaded with meat, market hall, diners.*

### #8 — The Rambla of Montevideo
The soul of the capital is its waterfront. The Rambla is one of the longest
continuous promenades in the world, stretching for over 20 kilometres along the
river. Locals stroll it at sunset sipping mate from a gourd — the everyday ritual
that defines Uruguayan life.
*B-roll: sunset promenade, people drinking mate, riverside.*

### #7 — Casapueblo (Punta Ballena)
An artwork you can walk through. Casapueblo is a dazzling white, hand-sculpted
building tumbling down a cliff to the sea, created over decades by artist Carlos
Páez Vilaró. Part gallery, part hotel, it's most magical at sunset, when a daily
ceremony marks the sun sinking into the Atlantic.
*B-roll: white cliffside building, sea views, sunset ceremony.*

### #6 — Cabo Polonio
A village frozen in time. Cabo Polonio has no roads, no mains electricity and no
running water — you reach it by 4x4 across the dunes. What you get is wild beaches,
a lonely lighthouse and one of the largest sea lion colonies on the coast. Pure
off-grid escape.
*B-roll: 4x4 over dunes, lighthouse, sea lion colony.*

### #5 — Punta del Diablo
A bohemian fishing village turned laid-back beach haven near the Brazilian
border. Punta del Diablo has colourful wooden cabins, surf beaches and a relaxed,
artsy vibe. It's where Uruguayans go to switch off — rugged, friendly and utterly
charming.
*B-roll: fishing boats, surf beach, wooden cabins.*

### #4 — Colonia del Sacramento
Step back in time on the river. This UNESCO-listed colonial town was founded by
the Portuguese in 1680, and its Barrio Histórico is a dreamy maze of cobblestone
streets, old lighthouses and vintage cars. Just across the water from Buenos
Aires, it's pure romance.
*B-roll: cobblestone Street of Sighs, lighthouse, vintage cars.*

### #3 — Ciudad Vieja & Plaza Independencia (Montevideo)
The historic core of the capital. Plaza Independencia links the old city and the
new, watched over by the statue and mausoleum of national hero Artigas and the
elegant Palacio Salvo. From here you wander into Ciudad Vieja's colonial streets,
theatres and cafés.
*B-roll: Palacio Salvo, Artigas statue, old city streets.*

### #2 — La Mano (Punta del Este)
Uruguay's most photographed sculpture. "Los Dedos" — giant fingers rising out of
the sand as if a hand is emerging from beneath the beach — has become the symbol
of Punta del Este. Simple, surreal and impossible to walk past without a photo.
*B-roll: the hand sculpture, beach crowds posing, sunset.*

### #1 — Punta del Este
And our number one is South America's most glamorous beach resort. Punta del Este
is where the river meets the ocean, with chic marinas, high-rise skylines and
golden beaches that draw the jet set every summer. Calm bay on one side, wild surf
on the other — it's the dazzling heart of the Uruguayan coast.
*B-roll: marina and skyline, beaches, nightlife, aerial of the point.*
