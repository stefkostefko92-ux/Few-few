## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
South Africa — the "Rainbow Nation" that hosted the World Cup in 2010, and one of
the most spectacular countries on Earth. Big-five safaris and a flat-topped
mountain, sweeping coastlines, wine valleys and powerful history. Here are the Top
10 landmarks you have to see in South Africa. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Table Mountain, safari animals, coastline, multicolour flags.*

### #10 — Robben Island
A place of history and hope. Off the coast of Cape Town lies the island prison
where Nelson Mandela spent 18 of his 27 years behind bars. Tours led by former
prisoners tell the story of the struggle against apartheid. Deeply moving and
essential.
*B-roll: island and prison, Mandela's cell, ferry, Cape Town skyline behind.*

### #9 — Boulders Beach Penguins
Penguins on a sunny beach. At Boulders Beach near Simon's Town, a colony of African
penguins waddles among giant granite boulders and turquoise coves — and you can
swim right alongside them. Adorable and unique.
*B-roll: penguins on the sand, boulders, swimmers nearby.*

### #8 — The Garden Route
One of the world's great road trips. The Garden Route winds along the south coast
past lagoons, forests, dramatic cliffs and beach towns like Knysna and Plettenberg
Bay, with whales offshore and adventure at every stop. Scenic perfection.
*B-roll: coastal road, lagoons, forests, whales.*

### #7 — The Blyde River Canyon
One of the largest green canyons in the world. The Blyde River Canyon plunges
through lush subtropical hills, with iconic viewpoints like the Three Rondavels and
God's Window, and the swirling rock pools of Bourke's Luck Potholes. Breathtaking.
*B-roll: Three Rondavels, canyon panorama, rock pools.*

### #6 — The Cape Winelands
World-class wine and mountain scenery. Around the historic towns of Stellenbosch and
Franschhoek, vineyards roll between dramatic peaks, dotted with Cape Dutch manor
houses. Tour the estates and taste some of the finest wines in the Southern
Hemisphere.
*B-roll: vineyards and mountains, Cape Dutch architecture, wine tasting.*

### #5 — The Cape of Good Hope
Where two oceans seem to meet. At the tip of the Cape Peninsula, rugged cliffs drop
into the sea at this legendary headland, framed by fynbos, baboons and dramatic
ocean views. A wild and historic spot at the bottom of Africa.
*B-roll: cliffs and ocean, lighthouse, the famous signpost.*

### #4 — The Drakensberg
The "Dragon Mountains." This vast UNESCO mountain range soars in jagged
basalt walls and green valleys, laced with waterfalls, hiking trails and ancient
San rock art. Some of the most majestic scenery in the country.
*B-roll: amphitheatre cliffs, valleys, waterfalls, rock paintings.*

### #3 — The V&A Waterfront & Cape Town
The vibrant heart of the Mother City. Cape Town's V&A Waterfront buzzes with
markets, restaurants and a giant Ferris wheel against a backdrop of harbour and
mountain. The city itself — colourful Bo-Kaap, beaches and nightlife — is endlessly
photogenic.
*B-roll: waterfront, harbour, colourful Bo-Kaap houses, beaches.*

### #2 — Kruger National Park
One of the greatest safari destinations on Earth. Kruger is a wilderness the size
of a small country, where you can spot the "Big Five" — lion, leopard, elephant,
rhino and buffalo — along with giraffes, zebras and so much more. The ultimate
African safari.
*B-roll: lions, elephants, giraffes at sunset, safari vehicle.*

### #1 — Table Mountain
And our number one is the icon of South Africa. Table Mountain rises flat-topped
above Cape Town, often draped in a "tablecloth" of cloud. Ride the rotating
cableway or hike up for a jaw-dropping view over the city, the ocean and the Cape
Peninsula. One of the New Seven Wonders of Nature. If you see one thing in South
Africa, make it this.
*B-roll: cableway up, flat summit, city and ocean panorama, cloud rolling over.*
