## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
In 2026 the United States hosts the biggest World Cup in history — 48 teams
across cities from coast to coast. But America is more than its stadiums. It's a
whole continent of canyons, skylines, neon and natural wonders. Before kickoff,
here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in the USA. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — skyline, canyon, falls, fans with USA flags.*

### #10 — Golden Gate Bridge (San Francisco)
We start on the West Coast with one of the most photographed bridges on Earth.
The Golden Gate stretches nearly three kilometres across the entrance to San
Francisco Bay, its famous "International Orange" towers often piercing through
rolling fog. When it opened in 1937 it was the longest suspension bridge in the
world. Walk it, bike it, or just watch it disappear into the mist.
*B-roll: drone along the towers, fog rolling under the deck, city skyline behind.*

### #9 — The Las Vegas Strip (Nevada)
From fog to neon. The Las Vegas Strip is six kilometres of pure spectacle in the
middle of the Mojave Desert — replicas of Paris, Venice and ancient Egypt, dancing
fountains, and lights you can see from space. Whether you gamble or not, the sheer
scale of it is a landmark in itself. This is America turned all the way up.
*B-roll: night drive down the Strip, Bellagio fountains, neon signs.*

### #8 — Niagara Falls (New York)
On the border with Canada thunders one of the most powerful waterfalls on the
planet. Over three thousand tonnes of water crash over Niagara every second. Ride
the Maid of the Mist boat into the spray, feel the roar, and watch rainbows form
in the mist. It's raw natural power just a short drive from the cities of the
Northeast.
*B-roll: boat in the spray, aerial of Horseshoe Falls, rainbow over the water.*

### #7 — Mount Rushmore (South Dakota)
Carved into the granite of the Black Hills are the 18-metre faces of four
American presidents — Washington, Jefferson, Roosevelt and Lincoln. It took
fourteen years and four hundred workers to sculpt this colossal monument to U.S.
history. Standing beneath it, you realise just how enormous each face really is.
*B-roll: wide shot of the four faces, close-up details, the approach avenue.*

### #6 — Yellowstone National Park (Wyoming)
The world's first national park is a land that looks like another planet.
Yellowstone sits on top of a giant volcano, and that energy bubbles to the
surface as steaming hot springs, mud pots and geysers — including Old Faithful,
which erupts skyward like clockwork. Add bison, bears and wolves roaming free,
and you have America's wild heart.
*B-roll: Old Faithful erupting, Grand Prismatic Spring from above, bison herd.*

### #5 — Walt Disney World (Florida)
The most visited holiday resort on Earth. Near Orlando, Walt Disney World is a
city of theme parks bigger than some countries' capitals, crowned by the fairytale
spires of Cinderella Castle. For families travelling to the World Cup, it's the
ultimate add-on — pure magic, and unmistakably American pop culture.
*B-roll: Cinderella Castle, fireworks, monorail, crowds on Main Street.*

### #4 — Times Square & New York City
The crossroads of the world. Times Square dazzles with towering digital
billboards and a river of people 24 hours a day. It's the beating, glowing heart
of New York — a city of yellow cabs, Central Park, Broadway and skyscrapers that
defined the modern skyline. No American trip is complete without it.
*B-roll: Times Square billboards at night, taxis, Central Park aerial, skyline.*

### #3 — Statue of Liberty (New York)
For over a century, she has welcomed arrivals to America. A gift from France, the
Statue of Liberty stands 93 metres tall from base to torch on her own island in
New York Harbor. Lady Liberty is a global symbol of freedom and the American
dream — climb to the crown for one of the best views in the country.
*B-roll: ferry approach, statue against the skyline, view from the crown.*

### #2 — The National Mall & White House (Washington, D.C.)
The capital's grand open-air museum. Along the National Mall you'll find the
soaring Washington Monument, the seated giant of the Lincoln Memorial, the U.S.
Capitol's white dome, and — behind its famous gates — the White House itself,
home to American presidents since 1800. It's history, power and architecture all
in one walkable stretch.
*B-roll: Washington Monument reflecting pool, Lincoln Memorial, the White House.*

### #1 — The Grand Canyon (Arizona)
And our number one is simply one of the natural wonders of the world. The Grand
Canyon is a mile deep, hundreds of kilometres long, and so vast it bends your
sense of scale. Carved over millions of years by the Colorado River, its layered
red cliffs glow at sunrise and sunset like nothing else on Earth. If you see one
landmark in America, stand on this rim.
*B-roll: sunrise over the canyon, river far below, helicopter fly-over.*

French Quarter of New Orleans; and the otherworldly rock formations of Monument
Valley.
