## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
South Korea — a country where ancient and ultramodern live side by side. Royal
palaces beneath glass skyscrapers, mountain temples and volcanic islands, K-pop,
street food and futuristic cities. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in
Korea. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Seoul palace and skyline, Jeju coast, lanterns, taegeuk flags.*

### #10 — Lotte World Tower, Seoul
We start with a modern giant. Soaring 555 metres over Seoul, the Lotte World Tower
is one of the tallest buildings on Earth, with a glass-floored observation deck near
the top. The gleaming symbol of Korea's high-tech capital.
*B-roll: the tapering tower, glass-floor view, city below.*

### #9 — Haedong Yonggungsa, Busan
A temple by the sea. Unusually for Korea, this beautiful Buddhist temple sits right
on the rocky coast near Busan, with shrines and pagodas perched above the crashing
waves. Stunning at sunrise.
*B-roll: seaside temple, pagodas over the waves, sunrise.*

### #8 — The DMZ
The world's most heavily fortified border. The Demilitarized Zone between North and
South Korea can be visited on guided tours — peering across into the North, exploring
infiltration tunnels and observation posts. A sobering, fascinating piece of living
history.
*B-roll: border observation deck, the truce village, fences (respectful tone).*

### #7 — Bukchon Hanok Village, Seoul
Old Korea in the heart of the city. Bukchon is a hillside neighbourhood of
beautifully preserved traditional hanok houses with curved tiled roofs, set between
two grand palaces — with city skyscrapers peeking beyond. A photogenic step back in
time.
*B-roll: tiled hanok roofs, narrow lanes, skyline beyond.*

### #6 — Changdeokgung & the Secret Garden
A palace in harmony with nature. This UNESCO-listed royal palace is famous for its
"Secret Garden," a tranquil landscape of pavilions, ponds and ancient trees designed
to blend with the natural hills. The most beautiful of Seoul's palaces.
*B-roll: palace halls, the garden pavilion and pond, autumn colours.*

### #5 — Jeonju Hanok Village
The home of Korean tradition — and food. Jeonju's large hanok village lets you stay
in traditional houses, wear hanbok and taste the famous bibimbap. The cultural soul
of Korean heritage.
*B-roll: hanok rooftops, hanbok visitors, bibimbap.*

### #4 — Seoraksan National Park
Korea's most beautiful mountains. Seoraksan bursts with jagged granite peaks,
temples, waterfalls and forests that blaze red and gold each autumn. Cable cars and
trails lead to breathtaking views. Nature at its finest.
*B-roll: granite peaks, autumn forest, mountain temple, cable car.*

### #3 — Bulguksa Temple, Gyeongju
A masterpiece of the ancient Silla kingdom. In Gyeongju — a "museum without walls" —
the UNESCO Bulguksa temple dazzles with stone terraces, pagodas and bridges from
over a thousand years ago. The heart of Korea's ancient history.
*B-roll: temple terraces, stone pagodas, the grotto Buddha nearby.*

### #2 — Jeju Island
Korea's volcanic paradise. Jeju is a subtropical island of lava tubes, waterfalls,
black-sand beaches and the dramatic Seongsan Ilchulbong "Sunrise Peak" — a giant
volcanic crater rising from the sea. A UNESCO natural wonder and the nation's
favourite holiday escape.
*B-roll: Sunrise Peak crater, waterfalls, coast, women divers.*

### #1 — Gyeongbokgung Palace, Seoul
And our number one is the grand royal palace of Korea. Gyeongbokgung, the largest of
the Joseon dynasty palaces, spreads beneath the mountains in the heart of Seoul, its
ornate gates guarded by costumed soldiers and skyscrapers rising behind. The
changing of the guard and a stroll in hanbok make it magical. If you see one place
in Korea, make it this.
*B-roll: palace gate and guards, throne hall, mountain backdrop, hanbok visitors.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the colourful Gamcheon Culture Village tumbling down a Busan
hillside, and the lantern-lit canals and markets of Seoul's Cheonggyecheon stream.
That's the Top 10 landmarks of Korea. Which one would you visit first? Subscribe —
we're counting down every nation at the 2026 World Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: Gamcheon village, lantern stream, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
