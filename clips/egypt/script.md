## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Egypt — the land of the pharaohs and one of the greatest open-air museums on Earth.
Towering pyramids and colossal temples, hidden tombs and the timeless Nile flowing
through it all. Few countries hold so much wonder. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you
have to see in Egypt. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — pyramids, temples, Nile feluccas, red-white-black flags.*

### #10 — The Grand Egyptian Museum
We start with the greatest treasure house of all. Near the pyramids, the vast Grand
Egyptian Museum displays tens of thousands of artefacts, including the complete
golden treasures of Tutankhamun. The world's largest museum devoted to a single
civilisation.
*B-roll: grand galleries, Tutankhamun's gold mask, colossal statues.*

### #9 — Islamic Cairo & the Citadel
The medieval heart of the capital. Islamic Cairo is a UNESCO maze of ancient
mosques, markets and madrasas, crowned by the hilltop Citadel of Saladin and the
silver domes of the Mohamed Ali Mosque. Dive into the bustling Khan el-Khalili
bazaar nearby.
*B-roll: Citadel and alabaster mosque, bazaar, minarets at sunset.*

### #8 — Abu Simbel
Colossal temples saved from the waters. Carved into a cliff by Ramses the Great,
the four giant seated statues of Abu Simbel guard temples that were famously cut
apart and moved, block by block, to escape the rising Nile. Awe-inspiring in scale.
*B-roll: the four giant seated figures, temple interior, lake beyond.*

### #7 — The Red Sea Reefs
A different kind of treasure. Off resorts like Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh, the
Red Sea hides some of the most vibrant coral reefs on the planet — a diver's
paradise of colourful fish, turtles and crystal-clear warm water.
*B-roll: coral reef, colourful fish, snorkellers, turquoise water.*

### #6 — The White Desert
A surreal moonscape. In Egypt's Western Desert, wind has sculpted brilliant white
chalk into giant mushroom and animal shapes that glow under the stars. Camping here
amid the strange formations feels like another planet.
*B-roll: white chalk formations, desert camp, starry sky.*

### #5 — Philae Temple & Aswan
Beauty on the Nile. The graceful Temple of Philae sits on an island near Aswan,
Egypt's most relaxed city, where feluccas drift on a wide blue river dotted with
golden dunes and Nubian villages. Serene and stunning.
*B-roll: island temple, feluccas, colourful Nubian houses.*

### #4 — The Valley of the Kings
The royal necropolis. Hidden in the desert hills near Luxor, the Valley of the
Kings holds the elaborate underground tombs of pharaohs — including the famous tomb
of Tutankhamun — their walls still blazing with colourful paintings after thousands
of years.
*B-roll: tomb entrances in the valley, painted tomb walls, hieroglyphs.*

### #3 — Karnak & Luxor Temples
The world's greatest temple complex. At Karnak, the jaw-dropping Hypostyle Hall is
a forest of 134 colossal columns. Nearby, Luxor Temple glows golden at night,
linked by an avenue of sphinxes. Ancient Thebes at its most monumental.
*B-roll: giant columns of Karnak, avenue of sphinxes, Luxor Temple lit at night.*

### #2 — A Nile Cruise
The journey that ties it all together. Sailing the Nile between Luxor and Aswan —
on a cruise ship or a traditional felucca — is one of the world's great travel
experiences, drifting past temples, palm groves and timeless village life along the
banks.
*B-roll: cruise on the Nile, feluccas at sunset, riverside temples.*

### #1 — The Pyramids of Giza & the Sphinx
And our number one is the icon of Egypt and the only surviving wonder of the
ancient world. On the edge of Cairo, the Great Pyramid and its companions have
stood for 4,500 years, guarded by the enigmatic Sphinx. Standing before them, you
feel the full weight of human history. If you see one thing in Egypt — in the world
— make it this.
*B-roll: pyramids and Sphinx, camels, sunrise over Giza, scale shots.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the Mediterranean city of Alexandria with its great library and
fort, and the spiritual landscapes of Mount Sinai. That's the Top 10 landmarks of
Egypt. Which one would you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every
nation at the 2026 World Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: Alexandria fort, Mount Sinai sunrise, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
