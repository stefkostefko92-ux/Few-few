## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Ecuador may be one of South America's smallest countries, but it might pack the
most wonder per square mile. In a single trip you can stand on the equator, climb
a volcano, paddle the Amazon and meet giant tortoises in the Galápagos. Here are
the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Ecuador. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Quito old town, volcano, Galápagos wildlife, yellow flags.*

### #10 — La Mitad del Mundo
We start where the world divides in two. Just north of Quito, the Mitad del Mundo
monument marks the equator — latitude zero — where you can stand with one foot in
each hemisphere. A fun, quirky landmark that gives the country its very name:
Ecuador.
*B-roll: equator monument, the painted line, the globe sculpture.*

### #9 — Baños de Agua Santa
Ecuador's adventure capital. Tucked between green mountains and waterfalls, Baños
is famous for its "Swing at the End of the World," which launches you out over a
canyon, plus zip-lines, hot springs and the thundering Pailón del Diablo
waterfall nearby. Adrenaline with a view.
*B-roll: cliffside swing, Pailón del Diablo falls, hot springs.*

### #8 — Cuenca
Colonial elegance in the southern Andes. Cuenca's UNESCO-listed old town is a
graceful grid of cobblestone streets, red-tiled roofs and the blue domes of its
New Cathedral. Relaxed and beautiful, it's also the true home of the
misnamed "Panama" hat.
*B-roll: blue cathedral domes, cobbled streets, river walk.*

### #7 — Cotopaxi Volcano
One of the highest active volcanoes in the world, and a near-perfect snow-capped
cone. Cotopaxi towers over the central highlands at almost 5,900 metres. Hike its
lower slopes, mountain bike down, or simply admire that iconic glaciated peak
rising above the plains.
*B-roll: snow-capped cone, wild horses on the páramo, climbers.*

### #6 — The Devil's Nose Railway (Nariz del Diablo)
A feat of engineering through the Andes. This dramatic railway zig-zags down an
almost vertical mountainside, switching back and forth to descend a sheer rock
face. The ride is a thrilling journey through some of Ecuador's most rugged
scenery.
*B-roll: train on the switchbacks, sheer cliffs, valley views.*

### #5 — Otavalo Market
The most famous indigenous market in South America. Every day — and especially
Saturdays — Otavalo fills with colourful textiles, handicrafts, ponchos and
jewellery sold by the Otavaleño people in their traditional dress. A feast of
colour and culture in the northern highlands.
*B-roll: stalls of colourful textiles, traders in traditional dress.*

### #4 — Quilotoa Crater Lake
A volcano filled with impossible colour. Quilotoa is a water-filled caldera whose
lake shifts between emerald and turquoise depending on the light. Hike down to the
shore, paddle a kayak, or trek the rim for ever-changing views of this
breathtaking crater.
*B-roll: turquoise crater lake, rim hike, kayak on the water.*

### #3 — The Amazon Rainforest (Yasuní)
Ecuador's slice of the Amazon is one of the most biodiverse places on the planet.
In Yasuní National Park, jungle lodges let you spot monkeys, parrots at clay
licks, pink river dolphins and caimans. A small country with a giant, living
rainforest.
*B-roll: river through jungle, macaws at a clay lick, canoe.*

### #2 — Historic Centre of Quito
The best-preserved colonial old town in the Americas, and one of the very first
UNESCO World Heritage Sites. At 2,850 metres, Quito's centre dazzles with baroque
churches dripping in gold — like La Compañía — grand plazas and white-washed
streets, all framed by surrounding volcanoes.
*B-roll: golden church interior, plazas, rooftops with volcano backdrop.*

### #1 — The Galápagos Islands
And our number one is a living laboratory of evolution. The Galápagos, 1,000
kilometres out in the Pacific, inspired Darwin's theory of evolution — and walking
among giant tortoises, marine iguanas, blue-footed boobies and sea lions that show
no fear of humans is unlike anywhere else on Earth. If you see one thing in
Ecuador, make it this.
*B-roll: giant tortoise, blue-footed booby, marine iguana, sea lions, snorkelling.*
