## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
DR Congo — through the intercontinental play-off and into the World Cup. The
vast, green heart of Africa is a land of staggering wildlife and raw natural power:
mountain gorillas, glowing lava lakes, snow-capped equatorial peaks and the mighty
Congo River. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in DR Congo. Let's count
them down.
*B-roll: montage — Nyiragongo lava, gorilla, Congo River, blue-red-yellow flags.*

### #10 — Kinshasa
We start in the capital. Kinshasa is one of the largest cities in Africa — a
sprawling, vibrant metropolis on the banks of the Congo River, famous as the
birthplace of Congolese rumba music and an unstoppable energy that fills its
streets, markets and music halls.
*B-roll: city skyline on the river, busy markets, live rumba.*

### #9 — Lola ya Bonobo
A sanctuary for our closest cousins. Just outside Kinshasa, Lola ya Bonobo is the
world's only sanctuary for orphaned bonobos — the gentle great apes found only in
DR Congo. Watch them play and swing in a forest haven. Heart-warming and unique.
*B-roll: bonobos in the forest, playing, caregivers.*

### #8 — Zongo Falls
A thundering curtain in the jungle. A few hours from Kinshasa, the Zongo Falls
plunge powerfully over a wide rock face into a misty pool surrounded by lush
rainforest. A spectacular and accessible natural escape.
*B-roll: wide waterfall, mist, rainforest, rainbow.*

### #7 — Lake Kivu
One of Africa's great lakes. On the eastern border, Lake Kivu is a beautiful
highland lake ringed by green hills and lakeside towns like Goma and Bukavu, with
islands, beaches and gentle waters. A scenic, relaxed contrast to the wild
mountains.
*B-roll: lake and green hills, lakeside town, islands, boats.*

### #6 — Boyoma Falls & the Wagenia Fishermen
A living tradition on the river. Near Kisangani, the Boyoma Falls are a series of
seven cataracts on the Congo River — where for generations the Wagenia people have
fished using huge wooden tripod traps wedged into the rapids. A remarkable sight.
*B-roll: river cataracts, wooden fishing tripods, fishermen at work.*

### #5 — Kahuzi-Biega National Park
Home of the eastern lowland gorilla. This UNESCO rainforest park, between two
extinct volcanoes, is the best place on Earth to see the rare Grauer's gorilla —
the largest of all the gorillas — trekking through dense, misty jungle.
*B-roll: lowland gorillas, dense rainforest, trekkers.*

### #4 — The Rwenzori Mountains
The legendary "Mountains of the Moon." This UNESCO range on the eastern border rises
to glaciers and snow-capped peaks right on the equator, with strange giant plants,
waterfalls and misty valleys. One of Africa's most extraordinary and challenging
treks.
*B-roll: snow peaks on the equator, giant lobelia plants, misty valleys.*

### #3 — The Congo River
The mighty heart of the country. The Congo is the second-largest river in the world
by flow and the deepest on Earth, snaking thousands of kilometres through rainforest.
A riverboat journey along it is one of the great epic adventures of Africa.
*B-roll: vast river through jungle, riverboats, aerial bends.*

### #2 — The Mountain Gorillas of Virunga
An unforgettable encounter. Virunga National Park — Africa's oldest national park —
shelters some of the world's last mountain gorillas. Trekking through the forest to
sit quietly metres from a gorilla family is one of the most moving wildlife
experiences on the planet.
*B-roll: mountain gorilla family, silverback, forest trek.*

### #1 — Mount Nyiragongo
And our number one is one of the most jaw-dropping sights on Earth. Nyiragongo, in
Virunga National Park, is an active volcano that holds the world's largest lava
lake — a churning, glowing cauldron of molten rock. Climbers camp on the crater rim
and watch the lava blaze red against the night sky. Simply spectacular. If you see
one thing in DR Congo, make it this.
*B-roll: glowing lava lake at night, crater rim camp, climbers, volcano cone.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the okapi — the elusive "forest giraffe" found only here — in the
Ituri rainforest, and the wide savannahs and wildlife of Garamba National Park.
That's the Top 10 landmarks of DR Congo. Which one would you visit first? Subscribe
— we're counting down every nation at the 2026 World Cup. See you in the next

*B-roll: okapi, Garamba savannah, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
