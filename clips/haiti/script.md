## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Haiti — the first free Black republic in the world, born from the only successful
slave revolution in history. It's a country of proud history, vibrant art and
striking natural beauty: mountaintop fortresses, blue lagoons and colourful towns.
Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Haiti. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Citadelle fortress, blue water, colourful art, blue-red flags.*

### #10 — The Iron Market, Port-au-Prince
We start in the capital. The Marché en Fer — the "Iron Market" — is a landmark
covered bazaar beneath distinctive red iron towers and arches, packed with crafts,
spices, art and the bustle of everyday Haitian life. The colourful heart of the
city.
*B-roll: red iron towers, market stalls, crafts and spices.*

### #9 — The Côte des Arcadins
Haiti's beach coast. North of the capital, the Côte des Arcadins is a stretch of
sandy beaches, turquoise water and resorts, with offshore reefs and islets perfect
for snorkelling and diving. The country's favourite seaside escape.
*B-roll: sandy beach, turquoise water, reefs, palms.*

### #8 — Saut-d'Eau Waterfall
A sacred cascade. Saut-d'Eau is a beautiful multi-tiered waterfall tumbling through
the forest — and one of the most important spiritual sites in Haitian Vodou, drawing
thousands of pilgrims to bathe in its waters each summer. Powerful and serene.
*B-roll: forest waterfall, bathers, lush greenery (respectful tone).*

### #7 — Pic la Selle
The roof of Haiti. Pic la Selle is the country's highest mountain, rising over
2,600 metres in the wild southeast. The hike up through pine forests rewards you
with sweeping views over Haiti and across the border. Cool, green and remote.
*B-roll: pine-clad mountains, summit views, trail.*

### #6 — Île-à-Vache
An idyllic island escape. Off the southern coast, Île-à-Vache is a peaceful island
of white-sand beaches, fishing villages and clear water, with no traffic and a
gentle pace of life. Pure tropical tranquillity.
*B-roll: white beach, fishing boats, island village.*

### #5 — Labadee
A Caribbean paradise on the north coast. Set on a lush private peninsula near
Cap-Haïtien, Labadee dazzles with turquoise coves, white sand and forested hills —
a tropical playground of beaches and watersports. Postcard-perfect.
*B-roll: turquoise coves, white sand, forested headlands, zip-line.*

### #4 — Bassin Bleu
Hidden blue pools. Near Jacmel, Bassin Bleu is a series of cobalt-blue waterfalls
and pools tucked in a forest gorge, reached by a short adventurous hike. Dive into
the cool, deep water surrounded by greenery. A magical secret.
*B-roll: blue waterfall pools, forest gorge, cliff jump.*

### #3 — Jacmel
The city of art. This charming coastal town is famous for its faded colonial
mansions with cast-iron balconies, its vibrant arts and handicrafts, and its
legendary carnival with giant papier-mâché masks. The creative soul of Haiti.
*B-roll: colonial balconies, art workshops, carnival masks, seafront.*

### #2 — Sans-Souci Palace
The "Versailles of the Caribbean." Built by King Henri Christophe, the ruins of
Sans-Souci Palace rise in grand terraces and staircases against a backdrop of green
mountains near Milot. Once a magnificent royal residence, now a hauntingly beautiful
UNESCO site.
*B-roll: palace ruins, grand staircases, mountain backdrop.*

### #1 — The Citadelle Laferrière
And our number one is the pride of Haiti and the largest fortress in the Americas.
Perched dramatically on a mountain peak, the Citadelle Laferrière was built after
independence to defend the new nation, bristling with hundreds of cannons and walls
40 metres high. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a soaring symbol of Haitian
freedom. If you see one thing in Haiti, make it this.
*B-roll: mountaintop fortress, cannons, walls, aerial above the clouds.*
