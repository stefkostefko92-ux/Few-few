## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Japan — a country of perfect contrasts. Ancient temples and neon megacities, silent
gardens and bullet trains, snow-capped Fuji and cherry blossom. Tradition and the
future, side by side. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Japan. Let's
count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Fuji, Tokyo neon, torii gates, cherry blossom, red-circle flags.*

### #10 — Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo
We start in the heart of modern Tokyo. Shibuya Crossing is the busiest pedestrian
crossing in the world, where hundreds of people surge across in every direction
beneath giant screens and dazzling lights. The pure, electric energy of the
Japanese capital.
*B-roll: crowds crossing from above, neon screens, night lights.*

### #9 — Osaka Castle
A samurai stronghold. Osaka Castle rises in tiers of green and gold above its moat
and stone walls, surrounded by a park that turns pink with cherry blossom each
spring. One of Japan's most famous and dramatic castles.
*B-roll: tiered castle, moat and walls, cherry blossom.*

### #8 — Himeji Castle
The "White Heron." Himeji is Japan's most beautiful original castle — a brilliant
white, elegant fortress that has survived for over 400 years. A UNESCO masterpiece
of Japanese architecture, especially stunning framed by blossom.
*B-roll: white castle, layered roofs, blossom, interior.*

### #7 — Nara's Deer & the Great Buddha
Where deer roam free. In Nara, hundreds of tame deer wander the parks and bow for
treats, beside the great Todai-ji temple — home to a colossal bronze Buddha
sheltered in one of the largest wooden buildings in the world. Charming and awe-
inspiring.
*B-roll: deer in the park, giant temple, bronze Buddha.*

### #6 — Itsukushima Shrine, Miyajima
The floating gate. On the sacred island of Miyajima, the great vermilion torii gate
of Itsukushima Shrine appears to float on the sea at high tide. One of the most
iconic and photographed sights in all of Japan.
*B-roll: floating torii at high tide, shrine, island and deer.*

### #5 — The Hiroshima Peace Memorial
A powerful symbol of peace. The skeletal A-Bomb Dome stands preserved as it was
after 1945, beside the moving Peace Memorial Park and museum. A solemn, essential
reminder and a message of hope for the world.
*B-roll: the A-Bomb Dome, peace park, eternal flame (respectful tone).*

### #4 — Kinkaku-ji, Kyoto
The Golden Pavilion. This dazzling Zen temple, covered in gold leaf, sits beside a
mirror-still pond that reflects it perfectly, framed by manicured gardens. One of
the most beautiful and serene sights in Kyoto, the old imperial capital.
*B-roll: golden temple reflected in the pond, gardens, autumn or snow.*

### #3 — Senso-ji & Tokyo
Tokyo's oldest temple. In the Asakusa district, the bright red Senso-ji temple is
approached through a giant lantern gate and a bustling street of traditional
shops. Old Tokyo charm in the heart of the world's biggest city — with the Skytree
rising nearby.
*B-roll: giant red lantern, temple, shopping street, Skytree.*

### #2 — Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kyoto
A tunnel of a thousand gates. At Fushimi Inari, thousands of vermilion torii gates
wind up a forested mountainside in a mesmerising orange tunnel. Walking through them
is one of the most magical experiences in Japan.
*B-roll: endless torii tunnel, fox statues, forest path, hillside view.*

### #1 — Mount Fuji
And our number one is the soul of Japan. Mount Fuji's perfect snow-capped cone rises
3,776 metres, sacred and instantly recognisable. Admire it mirrored in the Fuji Five
Lakes, framed by cherry blossom or a red pagoda, or climb it in summer to watch the
sunrise from the summit. The ultimate symbol of Japan. If you see one thing, make it
this.
*B-roll: Fuji over a lake, with blossom and pagoda, summit sunrise.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the snow monkeys bathing in hot springs at Jigokudani, and the
bamboo forest of Arashiyama. That's the Top 10 landmarks of Japan. Which one would
you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every nation at the 2026 World
Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: snow monkeys, bamboo grove, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
