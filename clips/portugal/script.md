## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Portugal — small in size but huge in beauty. This is a land of fairytale palaces
and golden cliffs, historic yellow trams, tiled facades and the deep blue
Atlantic. Once it set sail to map the world; today it's one of Europe's most loved
destinations. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Portugal. Let's
count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Lisbon trams, Algarve cliffs, Pena Palace, green-and-red flags.*

### #10 — Tram 28 & the Alfama, Lisbon
We start aboard Lisbon's most famous ride. The vintage yellow Tram 28 rattles up
and down the steep, narrow streets of the Alfama, the city's oldest neighbourhood —
a maze of tiled houses, hidden squares and the soulful sound of fado drifting from
the bars. The perfect introduction to Lisbon.
*B-roll: yellow tram on a steep street, Alfama alleys, fado singer.*

### #9 — The University of Coimbra
One of the oldest universities in the world. Founded in 1290, Coimbra's hilltop
campus is crowned by the breathtaking Joanina Library — a gilded baroque hall of
ancient books, even guarded by a colony of bats that eat the insects. Tradition
and beauty in one.
*B-roll: Joanina Library, university tower, student gowns.*

### #8 — Bom Jesus do Monte, Braga
A staircase to heaven. Above the city of Braga, the sanctuary of Bom Jesus do
Monte is famous for its monumental zig-zag baroque stairway, decorated with
fountains and statues, climbing the hillside to a beautiful church. The views over
the city are sublime.
*B-roll: the zig-zag stairway from above, fountains, hilltop church.*

### #7 — Óbidos
A medieval village wrapped in walls. Óbidos is a postcard of whitewashed houses
trimmed in blue and yellow, bougainvillea spilling over cobblestones, all enclosed
by ancient ramparts you can walk. Don't leave without a cup of ginjinha — cherry
liqueur served in a chocolate cup.
*B-roll: walled village, flower-lined lanes, ramparts walk.*

### #6 — Benagil Cave & the Algarve
The golden coast. The Algarve's cliffs glow ochre above turquoise water, carved
into arches, grottoes and hidden beaches. The crown jewel is the Benagil Cave — a
sea cave with a natural skylight opening onto a tiny beach, reachable only by water.
*B-roll: Benagil cave skylight, golden cliffs, kayaks, beaches.*

### #5 — The Douro Valley
The birthplace of port wine. The Douro winds through dramatic terraced hillsides
carved with vineyards for centuries. Cruise the river, ride the scenic train, or
sip wine at a quinta as the valley glows gold at sunset. One of the world's most
beautiful wine regions.
*B-roll: terraced vineyards, river cruise, wine terrace at sunset.*

### #4 — Belém Tower & Jerónimos Monastery, Lisbon
Monuments to the Age of Discovery. The ornate Belém Tower has guarded the mouth of
the Tagus for 500 years, while the nearby Jerónimos Monastery dazzles with
intricate Manueline stone carving. From here, Portugal's explorers once sailed to
map the world.
*B-roll: Belém Tower on the water, monastery cloisters, carved facades.*

### #3 — Ribeira & the Port Cellars, Porto
The soul of the north. Porto tumbles down to the Douro in a jumble of colourful
houses — the Ribeira district — linked to the port-wine cellars across the river by
the iron Dom Luís I Bridge. Add the blue-tiled São Bento station and you have one
of Europe's most atmospheric cities.
*B-roll: Ribeira waterfront, double-deck iron bridge, port cellars.*

### #2 — Sintra & the Pena Palace
A fairytale in the hills. Just outside Lisbon, the lush forested hills of Sintra
are dotted with romantic palaces — none more dazzling than the Pena Palace, a
riot of red, yellow and purple towers perched on a peak. Add the mysterious
Quinta da Regaleira with its spiral initiation well, and it feels enchanted.
*B-roll: colourful Pena Palace, misty hills, Regaleira's spiral well.*

### #1 — Historic Lisbon
And our number one is the capital itself. Built across seven hills above the
Tagus, Lisbon dazzles with tiled facades, grand plazas like the riverside Praça do
Comércio, the hilltop São Jorge Castle and viewpoints — miradouros — at every
turn. Sunlit, soulful and endlessly charming. If you see one place in Portugal,
make it Lisbon.
*B-roll: rooftops to the river, São Jorge Castle, miradouro at sunset.*
