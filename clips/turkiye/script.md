## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Türkiye — the bridge between Europe and Asia, and back at the World Cup through the
play-offs. A country of fairy-chimney valleys and ancient ruins, grand imperial
mosques, cotton-white terraces and a city that straddles two continents. Here are
the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in Türkiye. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Cappadocia balloons, Istanbul skyline, Pamukkale, red flags.*

### #10 — Antalya & the Turquoise Coast
We start by the sea. Antalya is the gateway to the stunning Turquoise Coast, where
the Taurus Mountains meet the Mediterranean in a string of beaches, waterfalls,
ancient ruins and sunken cities. A sun-soaked paradise.
*B-roll: turquoise coast, old harbour, waterfalls, beaches.*

### #9 — Mount Nemrut
Giant heads on a mountaintop. At the summit of Mount Nemrut stand the colossal
stone heads of gods and kings, scattered around a 2,000-year-old royal tomb. Watch
the sun rise or set over these mysterious, toppled giants. Eerie and unforgettable.
*B-roll: giant stone heads, mountaintop, sunrise.*

### #8 — The Library of Celsus, Ephesus
A window into ancient Rome. Ephesus is one of the best-preserved classical cities
in the world, crowned by the magnificent two-storey façade of the Library of
Celsus. Walk its marble streets past temples and a vast theatre where Saint Paul
once preached.
*B-roll: library façade, marble main street, great theatre.*

### #7 — Topkapı Palace, Istanbul
Heart of an empire. For four centuries, Topkapı was the opulent home of the Ottoman
sultans — a complex of courtyards, jewelled treasuries, the harem and dazzling tiled
pavilions overlooking the Bosphorus. Sumptuous and historic.
*B-roll: palace courtyards, tiled rooms, treasury, Bosphorus view.*

### #6 — The Grand Bazaar, Istanbul
One of the oldest and largest covered markets in the world. The Grand Bazaar is a
labyrinth of over 4,000 shops under painted arches, glittering with carpets, lamps,
gold, spices and ceramics. A feast for the senses — and a test of your bargaining.
*B-roll: arched lanes, lamps and carpets, spices, crowds.*

### #5 — Pamukkale
The "Cotton Castle." Pamukkale is a surreal hillside of brilliant white terraces,
formed over millennia by mineral-rich thermal water cascading down into turquoise
pools you can bathe in. Above sit the ruins of ancient Hierapolis. Otherworldly.
*B-roll: white travertine terraces, turquoise pools, bathers, ruins.*

### #4 — The Bosphorus, Istanbul
The strait that divides two continents. A cruise along the Bosphorus glides between
Europe and Asia, past Ottoman palaces, wooden waterside mansions, fortresses and the
great bridges linking the two sides of Istanbul. The classic way to see the city.
*B-roll: ferry on the strait, palaces, bridges, gulls and skyline.*

### #3 — The Blue Mosque, Istanbul
A masterpiece of Ottoman architecture. The Sultan Ahmed Mosque — the "Blue Mosque" —
dazzles with its cascade of domes and six slender minarets, and an interior glowing
with more than 20,000 hand-painted blue Iznik tiles. Serene and stunning.
*B-roll: domes and minarets, blue-tiled interior, courtyard.*

### #2 — Cappadocia
A landscape from a dream. Cappadocia is a magical region of "fairy chimney" rock
spires, cave churches and underground cities carved into soft volcanic stone. At
dawn, hundreds of hot-air balloons drift over the valleys in one of the most famous
sights on Earth.
*B-roll: balloons at sunrise, fairy chimneys, cave hotels, valleys.*

### #1 — Hagia Sophia, Istanbul
And our number one is one of the greatest buildings ever made. For nearly 1,500
years, Hagia Sophia has astonished the world — first a Byzantine cathedral, then an
Ottoman mosque, its colossal dome seeming to float on light, walls shimmering with
golden mosaics. A monument where two great empires and faiths meet. If you see one
thing in Türkiye, make it this.
*B-roll: the great dome, golden mosaics, vast interior, exterior with minarets.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro

would you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every nation at the 2026

*B-roll: underground city, Ölüdeniz lagoon, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
