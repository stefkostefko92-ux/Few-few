## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Australia — a whole continent packed into one country. The red heart of the
Outback, the world's greatest reef, golden surf beaches and some of the most
liveable cities on the planet. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in
Australia. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Sydney harbour, Uluru, reef, beaches, blue-and-stars flags.*

### #10 — Bondi Beach, Sydney
We start at the beach. Bondi is Australia's most famous stretch of sand, a golden
crescent of surf, sunbathers and the scenic Bondi-to-Coogee coastal walk. Pure
Aussie beach culture.
*B-roll: surfers, golden beach, coastal walk.*

### #9 — Melbourne & the Laneways
The cool cultural capital. Melbourne hides its charm in graffiti-covered laneways
packed with coffee shops, rooftop bars and street art, ringed by grand Victorian
architecture and sporting arenas. A city built for good living.
*B-roll: street-art laneways, trams, café culture, skyline.*

### #8 — Kakadu National Park
The wild Top End. Kakadu is a vast UNESCO wilderness of wetlands, waterfalls and
stone escarpments, teeming with crocodiles and birdlife, and home to ancient
Aboriginal rock art tens of thousands of years old. Raw and timeless.
*B-roll: wetlands, waterfalls, crocodiles, ancient rock art.*

### #7 — The Whitsundays
Islands in a turquoise dream. The Whitsunday Islands sit in the heart of the Great
Barrier Reef, fringed by the pure white silica sand of Whitehaven Beach and swirling
turquoise shallows. Paradise from above and below.
*B-roll: Whitehaven Beach swirls, sailing, islands from the air.*

### #6 — The Blue Mountains
A misty wonderland near Sydney. The Blue Mountains shimmer with a bluish haze from
the eucalyptus forests, dropping into deep valleys past waterfalls and the famous
Three Sisters rock formation. A beautiful escape from the city.
*B-roll: Three Sisters, blue-hazed valleys, waterfalls, cable car.*

### #5 — The Great Ocean Road
One of the world's great coastal drives. This famous route hugs the wild southern
coast past surf beaches and rainforest to the Twelve Apostles — towering limestone
stacks rising from the crashing sea. Spectacular at sunset.
*B-roll: coastal road, the Twelve Apostles, surf, sunset.*

### #4 — The Daintree Rainforest
The oldest rainforest on Earth. The Daintree, in tropical Queensland, has been
growing for over 180 million years — a dense jungle of ancient ferns and palms that
meets the reef at the coast, home to the rare cassowary. Living prehistory.
*B-roll: dense rainforest, ferns, river, where forest meets beach.*

### #3 — The Great Barrier Reef
The largest living structure on the planet. Stretching over 2,000 kilometres, the
Great Barrier Reef is a UNESCO underwater wonderland of coral gardens, turtles,
rays and thousands of fish — so vast it can be seen from space. A bucket-list dive
and snorkel.
*B-roll: coral reef from above, snorkellers, turtles, colourful fish.*

### #2 — Uluru
The sacred red heart of Australia. Uluru is a colossal sandstone monolith rising
from the flat desert of the Outback, glowing deep red and orange at sunrise and
sunset. Sacred to its Aboriginal custodians, it's one of the most spiritual and
iconic natural sights on Earth.
*B-roll: Uluru changing colour at sunset, desert, the rock up close.*

### #1 — The Sydney Opera House & Harbour Bridge
And our number one is the symbol of Australia. The Sydney Opera House, with its
billowing white sail-shaped roofs, sits on the harbour beside the great steel
"Coathanger" of the Harbour Bridge — which you can even climb. Together, framed by
the sparkling harbour, they're unmistakable. If you see one place in Australia, make
it this.
*B-roll: Opera House and bridge from the water, bridge climb, harbour at night.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the koalas and beaches of Kangaroo Island, and the pink lakes and
quokkas of Rottnest Island. That's the Top 10 landmarks of Australia. Which one
would you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every nation at the 2026
World Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: quokka selfie, Kangaroo Island wildlife, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
