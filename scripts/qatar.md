## Narration (voice-over script)

### Intro
Qatar — hosts of the 2022 World Cup and one of the most dazzling destinations in the
Gulf. Futuristic skylines and world-class museums, traditional souqs, golden desert
dunes and a glittering coastline. Here are the Top 10 landmarks you have to see in
Qatar. Let's count them down.
*B-roll: montage — Doha skyline, museum, desert dunes, maroon-white flags.*

### #10 — Katara Cultural Village
We start with culture by the sea. Katara is a purpose-built village of theatres, an
amphitheatre, mosques with shimmering tiled domes and art galleries along a beach.
The cultural heart of modern Doha.
*B-roll: amphitheatre, tiled mosque, beach, galleries.*

### #9 — Lusail
The futuristic new city. Built from scratch north of Doha, Lusail gleams with
striking towers, marinas and the spectacular Lusail Stadium that hosted the 2022
World Cup final. A glimpse of the future.
*B-roll: Lusail skyline, marina, the golden stadium.*

### #8 — The Pearl-Qatar
An island of luxury. This artificial island is designed around Mediterranean-style
marinas lined with yachts, upscale shops and pastel Riviera architecture.
Glamorous and photogenic.
*B-roll: marina with yachts, pastel waterfront, towers.*

### #7 — The National Museum of Qatar
A building like a desert rose. Designed by Jean Nouvel, the National Museum is a
stunning cluster of giant interlocking discs inspired by the crystal formations of
a desert rose. Inside, it tells the story of Qatar. Architecture as art.
*B-roll: the disc-shaped building, interior galleries.*

### #6 — Al Zubarah Fort
A window to the past. This restored 20th-century fort guards the ruins of a once-
thriving pearling and trading town, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site out in the
northern desert. Quiet, historic and evocative.
*B-roll: square desert fort, ruins, archaeology.*

### #5 — Souq Waqif
The soul of old Doha. Souq Waqif is a beautifully restored traditional market — a
warren of alleys filled with spices, textiles, handicrafts, shisha cafés and even a
falcon market. The lively, atmospheric heart of the city.
*B-roll: market alleys, spices, falcons, café terraces at night.*

### #4 — Khor Al Adaid, the Inland Sea
Where the desert meets the sea. A UNESCO-recognised natural wonder, Khor Al Adaid is
a tidal inlet where the Gulf reaches deep into towering dunes. Ride a 4x4 over the
sand to watch the sea lap against the desert. Spectacular.
*B-roll: dunes meeting the sea, 4x4 dune-bashing, sunset.*

### #3 — Aspire Park & the Torch
Green space and a soaring landmark. Aspire Park is Doha's largest green oasis,
overlooked by the 300-metre Torch Tower — built for the Asian Games — with its
sports complex and revolving views. The city's recreational heart.
*B-roll: green park, the Torch tower, families, fountains.*

### #2 — The Corniche & Doha Skyline
The classic view of Qatar. The Corniche is a sweeping seaside promenade curving
around Doha Bay, with the futuristic West Bay skyline glittering across the water —
best at sunset, when traditional wooden dhows drift past the towers.
*B-roll: skyline across the bay, promenade, dhows, sunset.*

### #1 — The Museum of Islamic Art
And our number one is Qatar's masterpiece. Designed by I. M. Pei and standing on its
own island, the Museum of Islamic Art is a stunning geometric building housing one
of the world's greatest collections of Islamic art — with breathtaking views of the
skyline from its park. Beautiful inside and out. If you see one thing in Qatar, make
it this.
*B-roll: the geometric building, atrium, skyline view, the park.*

### Honourable mentions & Outro
A couple more: the mysterious prehistoric rock carvings at Al Jassasiya, and the
mangroves of Al Thakira for kayaking. That's the Top 10 landmarks of Qatar. Which
one would you visit first? Subscribe — we're counting down every nation at the 2026
World Cup. See you in the next country.
*B-roll: rock carvings, mangrove kayaking, best-of montage, subscribe screen.*
