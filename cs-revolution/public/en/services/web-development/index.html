Our 5-phase process

1. Analysis and strategy

We start with a discovery call to understand your business, goals, target audience, competitors and technical requirements. Preparation of a detailed brief and strategy proposal.

2. Design and prototyping

We create wireframes and mockups in Figma. We iterate on the design with your feedback until final approval. Every screen is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile.

3. Front-end and back-end development

Implementation with clean, tested and documented code. Frontend in React/Vue/Next.js, backend in Node.js/Python/PHP where needed. Git with professional branch workflow.

4. Testing and launch

Testing on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, iOS, Android. SEO verification with Lighthouse, Schema.org validation, accessibility testing. After approval, we launch with active monitoring.

5. Support and optimization

Google Analytics, Search Console, Core Web Vitals monitoring. Monthly reports with continuous improvement suggestions based on real data.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a professional website cost?

Our websites start at €1,575 + VAT for showcase sites up to €5,000+ for complex e-commerce and corporate portals. Every quote is customized based on specific requirements. We respond within 24 hours with detailed analysis.

How long does it take to build a website?

A showcase site takes 2-3 weeks, a corporate site 2-4 weeks, an e-commerce 3-5 weeks, and enterprise projects 2-6 months. Times depend on complexity and feedback speed.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance contracts from €58/month + VAT that include security updates, daily backups, uptime monitoring, continuous SEO optimization, monthly reports and priority technical support.

Will the site be optimized for Google and AI?

Absolutely. Every site includes complete technical SEO, rich Schema.org, XML sitemap, optimized meta tags, Core Web Vitals below Google targets, and llms.txt for AI. Tested with Google Rich Results Test and Lighthouse.

Can I manage content myself?

Yes. We implement a CMS (WordPress, Strapi, Sanity or custom dashboard) that allows you to modify text, images, pages and blog posts without touching code. We also provide brief video training.

Does the site work in multiple languages?

Yes. We support native multilingual architectures with hreflang, localized URLs (/en/, /bg/), and multilingual CMS. All our projects are available in Italian, English and Bulgarian.

What happens if the site grows and becomes slow?

We constantly monitor performance. If the site grows beyond current hosting capacity, we automatically scale to more powerful VPS or implement advanced CDN/caching. Included in maintenance plans.
