Domande frequenti

How much does custom software development cost?

Depends on complexity. Simple internal tools start at €2,000, management dashboards from €5,000, complex systems with APIs and integrations from €10,000 up.

How long does software development take?

A working MVP requires 4-8 weeks. Complete enterprise systems 3-12 months. We follow agile methodology with incremental releases.

Who owns the code?

You do. At the end of the project we deliver all source code, documentation and credentials. No lock-in, no royalties.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Maintenance contracts from €200/month for bug fixes and support, from €1,000/month for continuous development of new features.

Do you integrate with our existing ERP?

Yes. We integrate with any ERP that has APIs (SAP, Odoo, Microsoft Dynamics) or via direct database and exchange files.

Will the software be on-premise or cloud?

Both supported. Cloud (AWS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean) for scalability, on-premise if your data must stay on-site for compliance.
