Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026 · version 1.1

The short version: your data never leaves your device.

What Déjà collects

Déjà extracts the text of the web pages you read so that you can later search them by meaning. That text, and the vectors computed from it, are stored exclusively locally, in your browser's IndexedDB.

What Déjà does NOT do

  • It does not send page content, URLs, search queries or any other data to servers — ours or anyone else's. There is no backend. No telemetry. No analytics.
  • It does not index mail, chats, login pages, payments or banking (a built-in denylist). Important: the denylist matches by address, not by topic — a page with sensitive content (e.g. a health article) on an ordinary site will be memorised locally. For such cases use the pause switch or add your own patterns in the settings.
  • It does not sell or share anything with anyone — there is nothing to share: your data never reaches us.

The only network request

On first run (and when the model is changed), Déjà downloads the embedding model files from Hugging Face. As with any network request, Hugging Face and its CDN see your IP address, an approximate IP-based location and standard HTTP headers — this is technically unavoidable when downloading a file. The request does not contain the content you read, your URLs, your searches or any indexed data. Hugging Face is a company based in the USA. If you do not want any external request at all, point the extension at your own mirror in Settings → “Custom model mirror” and Déjà will never contact Hugging Face. The model is cached locally and is not downloaded again.

Your controls

  • Pause — from the popup, stops indexing instantly.
  • Clear memory — irreversibly deletes everything memorised.
  • Automatic forgetting — optionally, Déjà deletes pages older than 3/6/12/24 months.
  • Removing the extension — deletes the database along with it.
