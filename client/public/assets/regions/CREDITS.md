## Re-encoding

The original Wikimedia Commons files are TIFF-sized scans of museum
photography (90 MB peak for the Corot). Each was centre-cropped to
1440 × 900, JPEG q80, 4:2:0 chroma, progressive (interlaced),
`-strip`'d of EXIF — so the full region set ships under 1 MB total.
