## Re-encoding

The original Wikimedia Commons files are TIFF-sized scans of museum
photography (4-50 MB each). Each was re-saved at 1920px wide, JPEG q78,
4:2:0 chroma, progressive (interlaced), `-strip`'d of EXIF — to keep
the entire backdrop set under 6 MB while preserving full-frame detail.
