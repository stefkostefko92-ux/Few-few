# Indexable pages: `/` with its language versions `/?lang=it` and
# `/?lang=bg`, plus `/terms` and `/privacy` (all listed in the sitemap).
# `/login` and `/register` stay crawlable ON PURPOSE — they carry
# `noindex`, and a crawler can only see that tag if it may fetch the page.
# The authed app (`/app/*`) and admin (`/admin/*`) are explicitly
# disallowed for every crawler. Search robots, answer-engine robots and
# AI-training robots are split below so we can opt in to citations while
# opting out of training corpora.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /app/
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /api/
