The Rhuddlan source was trimmed to a 25 s arc, re-scaled to 1280 × 720,
slightly desaturated, and crushed in the shadows to match the
gold-on-black palette of the rest of the UI. The forge source was
trimmed to an 18 s window starting 30 s in, centre-cropped to a 21:9
cinematic stripe at 1280 wide, slightly desaturated and contrast-lifted
to match. Audio was discarded in both clips (autoplay requires `muted`).
