You're protected

Supreme AdBlock is now blocking ads and trackers on every site you visit. There is nothing to set up.

  1. Pin it to the toolbar Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin Supreme AdBlock. One click then shows what was blocked on the page.
  2. If a site looks broken Open the extension and switch blocking off for that site, or choose "Site broken?" for quick fixes.
  3. Your browsing stays on your device Filtering happens locally. The extension collects no personal data and sends nothing about the pages you visit.
