Supreme AdBlock press fact sheet

Supreme AdBlock is a free ad blocker for Chrome and other Chromium browsers, developed in the EU, with no account and no telemetry. Everything below is checked against the product and this website. Last updated 23 October 2026.

How to describe it

Use this line: developed in the EU, no account, no telemetry.

Please do not write that it is the only European ad blocker. It is not, and we do not claim it.

Established Publisher is a Chrome Web Store badge. Google's documentation says it goes to publishers whose identity is verified and who keep a consistent positive record with Google services. It cannot be bought. Source: Chrome Web Store, discovery and badges, checked 23 October 2026.

What it does not do

  • It does not ask you to make an account.
  • It does not send analytics or telemetry. The one automatic request to us is the filter update, about twice a day.
  • It is not a VPN and does not hide your IP address from the sites you visit.
  • It does not remove every ad. Tell us about a missed one on the support page.
  • We state no user numbers on this page.
