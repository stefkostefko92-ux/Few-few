Supreme AdBlock was removed

Thank you for trying it. What it stored on this device was removed with it. If you left because something went wrong, it usually has a fix that takes one click, and you can keep blocking everywhere else.

  • A site broke

    In the popup, choose Site broken? Fix it or tell us, then Allow ads here for 1 hour, or switch Block ads on this site off. Only that site changes. More fixes for a broken site.

  • A YouTube video stopped playing

    If YouTube refuses ad blockers, Supreme AdBlock steps aside on YouTube for six hours by itself, so the video plays. Running two ad blockers at once can also stall the player. What to try on YouTube.

  • Something was hidden that you wanted to see

    The Focus mode switches, such as newsletter pop-ups, chat widgets or YouTube Shorts, are off until you turn them on, and you switch them off again in Settings. An element you hid with Hide element comes back with the ↶ button next to it.
