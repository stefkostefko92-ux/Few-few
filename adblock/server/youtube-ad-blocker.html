YouTube ad blocker for Chrome

Supreme AdBlock removes YouTube's ads before the player reads them. Video ads never start, the feed and search results lose their sponsored slots, and the video you chose loads as usual.

Works on youtube.com and in YouTube players embedded on other sites, in Chrome, Edge and Brave.

What it removes on YouTube

  • Video ads before and during a video, skippable or not.
  • Sponsored slots in the home feed, in search results and in the column of suggested videos.
  • Promoted banners and shelves on the home page, and the ad panels beside the player.
  • The same ads in embedded players, when another site shows a YouTube video.
  • If you want, in Focus mode: YouTube Shorts — the shelves, the tab and the suggestions.

YouTube ad blocking is on from the moment the extension is installed. There is nothing to set up.

Why videos keep playing

Many ways of blocking YouTube ads stop requests, or skip an ad once it has started, and YouTube watches for exactly that. Supreme AdBlock avoids the signals it looks for:

  • The video servers are never blocked, and neither are the reports a normal player sends while you watch. A player that goes silent is one of the things YouTube counts against you.
  • The ads are renamed, not cut out. The change is made to the player's data as text, before the player reads it, so everything else arrives exactly as YouTube sent it.
  • Nothing in the page gives it away. The browser functions it works through still describe themselves as the browser's own.
  • No tricks that YouTube counts. If an ad still slips through, the extension mutes it and presses YouTube's own Skip button. It never plays an ad at 16× speed.
  • No waiting penalty. When YouTube adds its 17-second pause before a video, the wait is cut to a blink.

If YouTube still says no

On some accounts YouTube shows an “ad blockers are not allowed” message and stops playing videos. When that happens, Supreme AdBlock steps aside on YouTube for six hours and reloads the page, so the video plays, with ads, instead of leaving you with a dead player. Settings shows YouTube bypass: ACTIVE until the six hours are over; then blocking resumes by itself.

You can also switch YouTube ad blocking off in Settings at any time. The rest of the protection keeps working.

What no extension can block

Some YouTube ads are stitched into the video stream itself, on YouTube's servers. Once an ad is part of the video, no browser extension can take it out. Sponsor messages that creators record in their own videos are part of the video too.

If you want YouTube without ads and want creators paid for your views, YouTube Premium is YouTube's own way to do that.

Kept up to date between store releases

YouTube renames its ad fields and page elements often, and a store review takes days. Supreme AdBlock downloads signed filter data about twice a day, with the names of new ad fields, ad panels and Skip buttons, so a change on YouTube can be answered within hours. It is data only: the code that uses it ships inside the extension and never changes from the network.

Block YouTube ads in Chrome in three steps

  1. Open Supreme AdBlock on the Chrome Web Store and choose Add to Chrome. It works the same way in Edge and Brave.
  2. Reload any YouTube tab that was already open, so the extension can start in it.
  3. Play a video. The popup shows how many requests were blocked on the page.

Questions about YouTube

Is the YouTube ad blocker free?

Yes. It is part of Supreme AdBlock, which is free, with no account and no paid tier.

Does it work with Manifest V3?

Yes. Supreme AdBlock was built for Manifest V3, Chrome's current extension platform, so the end of the old platform does not affect it.

Can YouTube stop me from watching?

YouTube says ad blockers go against its Terms of Service. What it does about them is show a warning and, on some accounts, stop playing videos. Supreme AdBlock avoids the signals YouTube looks for, and if playback is refused anyway it steps aside on YouTube for six hours so your videos play.

Does it work in the YouTube app or on a TV?

No. It works on youtube.com in your desktop browser and in YouTube players embedded on other sites. A browser extension cannot reach the YouTube apps on phones, tablets or TVs.

Does it see what I watch?

No. The change happens inside the page, on your device. Nothing about the videos you watch is stored or sent anywhere.

A video won't play. What should I do?

Reload the page first. If two ad blockers are switched on, turn one off: two blockers working on YouTube at once can stall the player. The support page has the other steps.
