Peter Lowe's ad and tracking server list — the author's terms

The author publishes no formal licence for the list. The list's home page,
https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/ , says (checked 23 October 2026):

  "Feel free to combine this list with yours or lists from other sites and put
  it up on the web, though!"

Supreme AdBlock combines the list with the other bundled lists, converted to
the browser's blocking-rule format, on that basis. The list's own header gives
these credits, kept here as the author wrote them:

  Peter Lowe - pgl@yoyo.org - https://pgl.yoyo.org/
