# Marketplaces and VAT in the EU — a quick guide (example external page)

Since 2015 platforms that sell e-services can be treated as the supplier for VAT. Article 9a of
Implementing Regulation (EU) 282/2011 sets a presumption: the platform acts in its own name unless the
real seller is explicitly named in the contracts, the invoice and the receipt. A platform that authorises
the charge, authorises the delivery or sets the general terms cannot name someone else as the supplier.
