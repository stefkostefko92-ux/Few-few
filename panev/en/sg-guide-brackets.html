Frequently asked questions

SG guide brackets: FAQ

Which sizes are available?

Widths 50, 60 and 80 mm and lengths 130, 150, 170, 190 and 220 mm: 15 codes, all in 4 mm sheet, priced on the list excl. VAT.

How do I read an SG code?

SG is the joining bracket between support and counterweight guide rail; the first number is the width and the second the length in millimetres. SG 80 150 = 80 mm wide, 150 mm long.

What do they combine with?

With the adjustable SU, SD and SC supports, or with wall fixing through the adjustable rigid arm.
