Frequently asked questions

Counterweight guide rail supports: FAQ

What is the difference between SU, SD and SC supports?

SU is the universal support and SD the one with an offset base, for more freedom of positioning on irregular walls: both in 5 mm sheet, range up to 215 mm. SC is the sliding support, in 4 mm sheet, for the widest extension ranges, up to 235 mm.

What do I order for one configuration?

Two codes: the support (SU, SD or SC) and the SG joining bracket listed for that configuration, for example SU 220 160 with SG 80 150. Both are on the price list with their own price, excl. VAT.

How is the wall-to-rail distance adjusted?

The joining bracket slides on the support within the extension range given for each configuration — for example 45 – 155 mm for SU 220 160 with SG 80 150 — with a 35 mm overlap flap.
