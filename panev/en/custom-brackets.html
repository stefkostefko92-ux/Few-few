Frequently asked questions

Custom brackets: FAQ

When are custom solutions needed?

For non-standard mounting conditions: the projection “A” of the SC 50 170 support is defined on the site’s shop drawing; the SG 225 50 guide is modular (150 + 30 mm, 5 mm sheet).

What is wall fixing with a rigid arm?

An anchoring solution for SG guide brackets combined with an SC support through an adjustable rigid arm, for fixing directly to the corner walls of the shaft. It comprises the SN 60 65 corner bracket, the SN 65 200 square bracket and the BRACCIO 160 190.

How do I get a price?

Special solutions are quoted on request: send the drawing or the measurements to info@panevascensori.it and receive a quote for the job.
