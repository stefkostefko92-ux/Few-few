Frequently asked questions

Landing door brackets: FAQ

What are Panev landing door brackets made of?

Two parts: the A plate, the perforated horizontal part, and the B bracket, the slotted vertical part, of the same section (37, 45 or 65 mm). Angular adjustment is ± 8° on the 65 series and ± 7° on the 45 and 37 series; the sheet is 5 mm (65 and 45 series) or 4 mm (37 series).

Can I combine plates and brackets of different series?

No: the A plate fits only B brackets of the same section. The 37 / 45 / 65 series are not interchangeable.

Are the brackets right- or left-handed?

They are ambidextrous: available as a right-hand (DX) and a left-hand (SX) version. State the hand you need when you order.
