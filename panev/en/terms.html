Commercial terms

2026 price list — the valid commercial offer prevails.

Scope

Sales are reserved to professional operators (B2B) holding a VAT number. Orders are deemed accepted upon written confirmation by Panev Ascensori SAS.

Prices

Prices in euro, excl. VAT, per single unit, as per the 2026 price list. Items marked “on request” are quoted per job. Volume and recurring-supply discounts on request. Subject to change.

Shipping

Free across Italy for orders above €500 (excl. VAT). Standard delivery 2 – 5 working days; express 24 – 48 h on request.

Payment

Advance bank transfer; RIBA for regular customers with a commercial credit line; credit card. Electronic invoicing through the Italian Exchange System (SdI).

Warranty

2 years on manufacturing defects. Free replacement of non-conforming components reported within 15 days of delivery.

Compliance and industrial property

Products compliant with UNI EN 81, steel compliant with UNI EN 10025, suitable for UNI 10411 inspection. System protected by utility model patent No. 202023000002112 (UIBM), valid until 19/05/2033.
