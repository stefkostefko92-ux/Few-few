Installation video · SU 220 160 + SG 80 150

Counterweight guide rail installation with SU and SG: video in 9 steps

Duration 1:45 9 steps Left-hand (SX) and right-hand (DX) versions Italian captions, no speech

The SU 220 160 support with the SG 80 150 bracket is installed in 9 steps: plumb lines, marking, drilling, SU support fixed with two M10 anchors, SG bracket lightly tightened, rail plumbed 5 mm from the line, SG moved up to the rail foot, N1 clips, check and final tightening. SX and DX versions, extension range 45–155 mm.

Installation step by step

The time next to each step opens the video at that point.

  1. 0:04

    Plumb lines

    Drop a plumb line from the top for each rail: it will hang 5 mm from the rail head.

  2. 0:12

    Marking

    Hold the SU support against the wall, level and in line with the plumb line, and mark the holes through the slots.

  3. 0:23

    Drilling

    Drill the marked points with a hammer drill and a 10 mm bit.

  4. 0:30

    Fixing the SU supports

    Fix each SU support with two M10 anchors and large washers, then tighten the nuts.

  5. 0:44

    SG bracket on the support

    On top: M10 bolt with a large washer. Underneath: large washer, split lock washer and nut. Tighten lightly: the SG must still slide.

  6. 0:59

    Plumbing the rail

    Lower the rail in front of the SG and plumb it: 5 mm between the rail head and the line.

  7. 1:09

    SG forward

    Slide the SG 20 mm up to the rail foot.

  8. 1:14

    N1 clips

    Fit two N1 clips on the rail, without tightening. Behind the SG flange: large washer, lock washer and nut.

  9. 1:23

    Check and tightening

    Check with the spirit level that it is vertical, then tighten everything: the SG bolts and the clip nuts.

Summary for qualified installers: the installation drawings in the technical catalogue and the design of the installation prevail.
