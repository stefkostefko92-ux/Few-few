The Panev lift bracket patent: utility model No. 202023000002112

Yes, Panev brackets are patented: the support bracket is protected by utility model patent No. 202023000002112, granted by the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (UIBM) on 7 January 2025 and valid until 19 May 2033.

What it protects

The Panev Ascensori support bracket is protected by a utility model patent granted by the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (UIBM). The title protects the adjustable fixing solution for structural components of lift and goods-lift installations.

The Panev bracket joins a wall-fixing element and a component-support element through an articulated joint that sets the angle, locked by a clamping element. The stiffening ribs are widest right next to the joint — precisely the most stressed area in service.

How the patented bracket is built

The parts of the system, as the patent describes them.

Fixing element
Fixed to the masonry of the lift shaft.
Support element
Carries the structural component, at right angles to the fixing element.
Articulated joint
Sets the angle between fixing and support.
Clamping element
Locks the angle once it is set.
Stiffening ribs
Folded edges, wider next to the joint.
Slotted fixing holes
Slots parallel to the edge, for fixing to the wall.
Connection holes
To fix the component to the support element.

Patent: FAQ

Are Panev brackets patented?

Yes. The Panev Ascensori support bracket is protected by utility model patent No. 202023000002112, granted in Rome on 7 January 2025 by the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (UIBM).

How long is the patent valid?

Until 19 May 2033. The application was filed on 19 May 2023.

What does the patent protect?

It protects the adjustable fixing solution presented in the catalogue: a wall-fixing element and a support element joined by an articulated joint, with a clamping element that locks the angle once it is set. Its title is “Staffa di supporto per un componente strutturale di un impianto di ascensore o montacarichi” — support bracket for a structural component of a lift or goods-lift installation (classification B66B).

Where can the patent be checked?

In the registers of the Italian Patent and Trademark Office (UIBM), under number 202023000002112. The certificate is reproduced on page 02 of the technical catalogue.
