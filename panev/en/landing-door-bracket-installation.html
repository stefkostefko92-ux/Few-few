Installation video · A 45 175 2 + B 45 320

Landing door bracket installation: video in 11 steps

Duration 2:13 11 steps Left-hand (SX) and right-hand (DX) versions Italian captions, no speech

Landing door brackets are installed in 11 steps: assemble plate A and bracket B, position them according to the distance from the car sill (25–32 mm), fix them with anchors, level the sill, tighten everything and trim the A plates flush with the sill. The video shows A 45 175 2 + B 45 320, in left-hand (SX) and right-hand (DX) versions.

Installation step by step

The time next to each step opens the video at that point.

  1. 0:04

    Assembling A + B

    Assemble plate A and bracket B with the bolts of the joint (13) and the clamping element (14), without tightening.

  2. 0:13

    Positioning the brackets

    Installer 1 holds one assembled bracket on the left, installer 2 the other on the right.

  3. 0:21

    Measuring from the car sill

    Measure the distance between the landing sill and the car sill (25–32 mm) and position the brackets accordingly.

  4. 0:30

    Marking, drilling and anchors

    Mark the drilling points through the slots (17), drill, insert the anchors and level each bracket B.

  5. 0:48

    Tightening the anchors

    Tighten the anchor nuts.

  6. 0:55

    Placing the sill

    Rest the sill, with or without the door frame, on the A plates.

  7. 1:01

    Light tightening

    Fix the sill to the connection holes (18) without fully tightening.

  8. 1:07

    Levelling the sill

    Level the sill along its length.

  9. 1:14

    Levelling plates A and tightening

    Rotate the A plates around the joint (13) until they are level, then tighten everything: joint (13), clamping element (14) and sill.

  10. 1:24

    Third bracket

    Install the third bracket in the middle of the sill. If you hit rebar while drilling, keep A DX and replace B DX with B SX: the holes move by 65 mm. Then mark, drill, anchor and tighten everything.

  11. 1:52

    Trimming the A plates

    Using a marker, mark each A plate flush with the sill, move the sill back, cut along the mark with an angle grinder and bring the sill back flush.

The numbers in brackets refer to the bracket parts: (13) joint, (14) clamping element, (17) fixing slots, (18) connection holes.

Summary for qualified installers: the installation drawings in the technical catalogue and the design of the installation prevail.
